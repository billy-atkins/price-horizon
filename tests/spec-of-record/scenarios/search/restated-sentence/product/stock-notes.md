# Stock Notes

## Around The Warehouse

The loading doors on the north side open at six, and the first lorry is usually waiting by then. Forklifts are charged overnight in the bay beside the office, which is why that bay is kept clear after four. The heating runs only in the packing room, since the main hall is too tall to warm. Whatever figure has gone into the book stays there for good, and an error is mended by adding its opposite beside it. Visitors sign in at the gatehouse and wear a yellow vest until they leave. The canteen closes at two on Fridays, when the weekend shift brings its own food. Pallets wrapped in blue film belong to the returns desk and are not shelved. The fire assembly point is the far corner of the car park, under the tall lamp.
