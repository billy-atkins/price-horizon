# Stocktake

A rule, and beneath it many short statements on the same subject, so a search by meaning for the rule has more than twenty places that share its words. `stock-notes.md` restates the rule in one sentence of a long passage on other subjects.

## The Rule

A count once entered is never overwritten: a miscount is put right by entering a second count that reverses the first.

## Counting

A count is taken at the close of each week by two counters.

A count entered after the week closes is marked as late.

A miscount found by the second counter is reported to the floor lead.

The first count of a new bin is entered by the counter who opened it.

A count of zero is entered as zero and never left blank.

Each count is entered against the bin it was taken from.

A count entered against the wrong bin is a miscount of both bins.

The second counter enters a count without seeing the first.

A count is entered in whole units, with no fractions.

A miscount of more than ten units is counted a third time.

The floor lead signs each week's counts once they are entered.

A count taken during a delivery is entered after the delivery ends.

A bin with no count entered for a week is counted first the next week.

Two counts of one bin that differ are both kept as entered.

A count entered by a trainee is checked by a counter the same day.

The counter enters the time each count was taken.

A count of a sealed pallet is entered from the label on its seal.

A miscount is never the fault of the counter who reports it.

Counts are entered on the floor, at the bin, and not from memory.

A count that reverses another names the count it reverses.

The first count of the year is entered for every bin, empty ones too.

A second count is entered on its own line, below the first.

A count entered for a bin that was moved names both places.

A miscount traced to a damaged label is entered with the label's number.

The week's counts are closed once the floor lead has signed them.

A count overwritten by mistake is reported as a fault of the book.
