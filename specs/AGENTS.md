# Spec of Record: Agent Instructions

This file is the entry point to Spec of Record for these specs. Read it before any work on a file in Spec of Record's scope (`specs/methodology/scope.md § What Spec of Record Governs`). It sends each change to the procedure that governs it, and it holds the rules that reach every author. Read `specs/methodology/glossary.md` next, before anything else (`specs/methodology/scope.md § Progressive Disclosure`). `specs/architecture.md` describes the specs as a whole.

## Writing specs

`specs/methodology/` holds the rules for writing specs and for the code the specs govern. Those rules, and the rules in this file, reach the files in Spec of Record's scope, as `specs/methodology/scope.md § What Spec of Record Governs` sets. The methodology's own files are among them.

**Keeping specs in sync.** What a change reaches beyond the facts it edits is kept in sync with those facts, as `specs/methodology/sourcing-and-citation.md § Keeping Specs in Sync` has it. What it reaches is each materialized view of the facts (`specs/methodology/sourcing-and-citation.md § Keeping Specs in Sync § Materialized Views`), and the counterpart that draws their subject from the other perspective (`specs/methodology/sourcing-and-citation.md § Keeping Specs in Sync § Product and Technical Counterparts`).

Every change a design makes is one of the kinds that `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document § The Kinds of Change` defines: a change to the canon's specs, to the application specs, to code, to the documentation, or to another file. A working file is of no kind of change, and is changed as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § The Working Files` has it. `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document` also sets when a change's design document declares its kind, and how a change that needs two kinds is split. Each change goes to the skill for its work. The skills for the specs serve every kind of spec alike, and each skill forks on the kind where the work differs:
- **Changing a spec, or another file in scope but code.** `.ai/skills/design-specs/`. Its Workflow gives each rule it applies a point-of-use citation, and forks into the reference for the change's kind.
- **Checking a spec, or such a file.** `.ai/skills/audit-specs/`. Its Workflow forks on the kind of each file it audits, and a run reads the audits and the semantic checks of the kinds it covers, as `specs/methodology/design-workflow.md § A Design's Reviews` sets. The skill that writes a plan checks the plan (`specs/methodology/scope.md § Rules and Skills`).
- **Designing and writing code.** `.ai/skills/implement-specs/`.
- **Checking code.** `.ai/skills/verify-spec-implementation/`.

Before doing by hand a kind of work that a registered skill covers, read that skill's `SKILL.md`.

Running the procedure, rather than recalling the rules, puts each rule in mind at the step it governs (`specs/methodology/sourcing-and-citation.md § One Home Per Fact`). This section's index finds a rule mid-step, whatever the work. The index routes. It stands in for no step of a procedure.

## Design, Refactor, Refine

This section sets how anything here is built or reworked, whether a spec, a rule, a skill, or code. The working files are aside: the builder changes them as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § The Working Files` has them changed. The builder (`specs/methodology/glossary.md`) takes the work through this process, in the order that `specs/methodology/design-workflow.md § The Order of a Design's Work` gives. `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Measure Twice, Cut Once` sets the builder's role against its reviewers. `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Decides` and `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Converging, or Not § The Runs` set when the builder stops for the user. The builder reads this opening again each time it resumes the work, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Reading Fresh` has a file read again. `.ai/skills/design-specs/` applies the process to the canon and the application specs, and `.ai/skills/implement-specs/` applies it to code.

### Reading Fresh

Before working on a file, read it again, however small the change or recent the last read. A direct edit is no exception. Nothing tells an agent whether its memory of a file survived compaction. Content written an hour ago feels remembered when it is a reconstruction.

### The Goals and the Frame

The builder and the user agree in discussion on at least one design goal for the change (`specs/methodology/working-files.md § Design Goal`). Each goal is evaluated against the change's problem, so that together the goals answer the problem and none reaches past it. No goal need ask for elegance (`specs/methodology/glossary.md`). Every design owes elegance, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Refactor`, `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Refine` and `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Measure Twice, Cut Once` have the builder carry it out.

Then, before drafting, the builder states the change's design frame (`specs/methodology/glossary.md`) and presents it to the user. The builder drafts only once the user agrees to the frame or adjusts it. Each answer is recorded as a steer (`specs/methodology/working-files.md § Steering Decision`). A steer that changes the goals or the frame is confirmed with the user before it is applied. The builder presents the change, with the frame re-evaluated against any goals that changed, and the user approves it before the work goes on. The design then takes a fresh adversarial review, unless the user's approval has ended its review. Any other steer is recorded as given (`specs/methodology/working-files.md § Steering Decision`). The frame's Approach and each of its Frame Outcomes rest on goals. The Approach cites the goals it rests on, as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § Design Goal` has a goal cited, and each Frame Outcome names them in its Goals (`specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document § Frame Outcome`). So a change to a goal finds what to re-evaluate. The frame's Scope, the problem space, and its Assessed Areas cite no goal. A frame the user never saw lets the builder solve a problem the user does not have.

The frame's scope, the cases the change governs, is the actual scope of the change. Too small a scope reaches a local optimum, right for the file in hand and wrong for the system around it. Only the real scope admits the global optimum. The sweep for it is research, done as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Measure Twice, Cut Once` has research done, and widened until nothing outside it would change the answer. Its Searches run by meaning as well as by the draft's words, because a search built from the draft's terms finds only what the draft already knew about. The sweep finds every passage stating part of each rule the change states, and every use of each term the change defines or redefines. It covers the files a review reads, as the opening of `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Checks What and Why` bounds them. The frame's scope is the cases the change governs among the areas the sweep's assessments find bearing on the design.

Each area the sweep's assessments find bearing on the design enters the frame as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document § Assessed Area` has an area enter it, or, its Impact holding `changes`, is named in the design's Out of Scope. Where an area goes is the builder's or the user's to decide, never the cold agent's. Where the builder would leave out an area whose Impact holds `changes`, it brings the assessment and its own reading to the user, whose answer is a steer (`specs/methodology/working-files.md § Steering Decision`). An area the user leaves out is named in the Out of Scope, citing the steer, and is taken up again only where the goals or the frame change. No such answer sets an Impact. An area is assessed again only as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Measure Twice, Cut Once` has an assessment stand. An area research turns up outside the frame takes the route `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Settling a Round § The Test` gives a place outside the frame. The frame's Scope records the sweep's method, and its Assessed Areas the areas in scope, never the whole of what the sweep matched.

The Frame Outcomes are settled before the draft, so that the draft is judged by the Frame Outcomes, rather than the Frame Outcomes fitted to the draft. Work the builder would add as the design goes on is held to the goals, as a review's findings are. Work that no goal reaches is a change of its own, however convenient folding it in would be. The builder hands that change to the user to spawn or log, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Design` has it, unless the user first widens the goals.

#### Whether the Goals and the Frame Name a Mechanism

This test asks whether the goals and the frame name a mechanism the steps change. Such a mechanism has states or routes, and the design alters its states, its routes or the facts deciding them, either in a step stating its rows or in keeping its text in sync. A rule written as prose that routes is such a mechanism. A mechanism whose wording alone the design keeps in sync, its decisions standing, is not. The builder asks the test at the trace, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Measure Twice, Cut Once` has it, and at settling, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Settling a Round § The Round at Its Causes § Group` has it. The check's report holds the table's outcome for each mechanism or decision the test is asked of, the self-review's in the trace of the mechanism and the round's in the cause continuing an earlier one, as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Check's Report` has each recorded. The user triages a design flaw the table finds, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Converging, or Not § A Design Flaw` has one triaged. Where the trace finds that the goals and the frame miss a mechanism, that design flaw is a call that blocks the adversarial round. It goes to the user when it arises, before that round, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Decides` has such a call asked.

### Design

Start from the problem to solve, stated plainly. Get a draft logically working, one that solves the problem correctly, before it is good. A plan describes the draft, and for the specs and for code, the plan is a design document. Where the work turns up a change of its own, that change is spawned as a design of its own or logged as a follow-up, as the user decides, and a change to working files alone is logged (`specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document`).

### Refactor

Make macro improvements to what is being built, to its internal workings and its external surface. These are structural changes, not wording, that make it better meet its actual needs and work toward elegance (`specs/methodology/glossary.md`). Repeat until Refactor converges, when a round leaves the structure where it was.

### Refine

Once Refactor has converged, make the elegance of what is built plain, and make it easy to use and to understand. Refine works on wording, naming, phrasing and consistency. Polishing something still moving structurally is wasted effort.

### Measure Twice, Cut Once

The builder and its reviewers pull against each other, as a software engineer and a quality assurance engineer do. The builder aims to hand a review nothing to find. The reviewer, in the adversarial review, the post-apply review and an audit alike, aims to find what the builder missed. That tension makes the work good. It holds only while the builder does its part, because quality comes from the builder's own passes, and the reviewers are a second set of eyes, never the first check. Fast and sloppy is slow, because each finding a reviewer makes sends the work back through the builder's process and can cost another review. Methodical and correct is fast. So the builder:
- builds the design top-down, as this section's paragraph on the order of a design's parts has it, and takes the steps through Design, Refactor, Refine before validation, and again whenever a change reaches a step, a steer's change among them, before the validation that precedes the next adversarial round; at the apply, it takes what it writes through them;
- zooms out to find how far the rule the change touches reaches, every other place stating the fact a fix changes among it. Before the plan's approval, the builder zooms out before it states a step's outcome and before it makes a fix to a step in settling a round, and that zoom-out is research, done as this section has research done. At the apply, the builder zooms out before it writes and before it makes a fix to text it wrote, reading fresh, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Reading Fresh` has a file read, each area assessed other than `none` that bears on what it writes: the areas the step's What names, which it changes, and the Assessed Areas holding `applies`, which it follows. That zoom-out is no research, and a Read Assessment, filtering for relevance, replaces none of its reading. Reading more costs only time and tokens, while reading too little costs the specs and what is built from them. The zoom-out reaches:
  - around: the parent of the section holding the change, whole, the section's siblings among it. Where the section is a top-level section, or the change sits in a file's text before its first section, it reaches that section or that text, with the sections on either side of it;
  - up: each ancestor's text before its first child heading, among them a file's text under its title, before its first section heading;
  - across: every passage elsewhere that cites what it reached around or any section within it, any ancestor of the section holding the change, or the file whole, a citation of one part by its key among them. It reaches each such passage around and up in turn, and no further;
  - in code or a script: the unit holding the change, the units it calls and the units that call it;
- names in a step's What, and in the Target, only the areas the frame's Assessed Areas hold with an Impact holding `changes`, as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § Design Plan Step` has a What name them, so that its own reading sets no part of the design. At the apply or in a fix, it writes the rule within them whole as one coherent statement, however much text that takes, never as a clause wedged into what stood. Patches laid one on another leave a rule in pieces no reader can follow. An area its research turns up outside the frame takes the route `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Settling a Round § The Test` gives a place outside the frame;
- holds each step, and what it writes, to the rules they apply, as it works;
- traces each mechanism with states or routes, such as a State Machine, a DAG or an Algorithm, through every path it allows, every way into each state of a State Machine among them: each mechanism its steps change, from the rows the steps state, before the adversarial review, and each mechanism the apply wrote, before the post-apply review. Before the adversarial review, it also takes each mechanism that `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § The Goals and the Frame § Whether the Goals and the Frame Name a Mechanism` reaches, those it traces among them, and asks whether the goals and the frame name it, as that section has it. It records the trace in its self-review's report, as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Check's Report` has a trace recorded. A path left untraced is the next review's finding, and finding it first costs less than the round that would find it;
- makes its self-review (`specs/methodology/glossary.md`) before each review that follows a change of its own, as its row of `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Checks What and Why` sets. Its work runs Design, Refactor, Refine, its self-review, the validation, then the adversarial review. At the apply, it runs the writing, its self-review, then the post-apply review;
- at the apply, tells apart each thing the user says, a steer, feedback on the applied result, direction or a decision about other work, by `specs/methodology/working-files.md § Steering Decision § Telling a Steer Apart`, before acting on it, and acts by its route. Where the user's words ask for a route the canon does not have, it says so and names the canon's route before acting.

**The order of a design's parts.** A design is read and built in the order its parts name each other: its goals, then its frame, its Scope and its Assessed Areas before its Frame Outcomes, then its steps, each after the steps its Depends On Steps names (`specs/methodology/working-files.md § Design Plan Step`). The builder drafts a design in that order. It makes each change at the highest part the change touches, whether the change is a steer's, a round's settlement, a pass's or the revision of what waited on another design. It then revises each part naming that part, down the same order, from the list the design script's `--show-dependents` gives of the parts naming or citing a part, a Scope paragraph named by its bold lead-in among them. A rule is stated once, at the highest part holding it, and each part below cites it. A part left unrevised below a changed one restates what the change has moved, and the next round finds it. A build tool works the same way, rebuilding each target after the targets it depends on.

**The design's inputs.** The user's steers and the Read Assessments of the design's research are the design's inputs, standing above it. They feed its goals, its frame and its steps, and no part of the design sets them. A steer changes only by the user's word (`specs/methodology/working-files.md § Steering Decision`). An assessment changes only by its Area being assessed again, as this section has an assessment stand.

**Research.** Reading and searching to find what a change reaches, or what the design must follow, are research (`specs/methodology/glossary.md`). Research sets no part of the design but through its assessments. The builder chooses what to research: its Searches, for a rule's reach, for the frame's sweep and for what restates a change, and the places it knows to read, by the reach of its zoom-out. It hands them to a cold agent, as the handed-off judgment's row of `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Checks What and Why` has research handed off, with the design document and a brief naming the research's cause. The cold agent records the places the builder named in its report's Reading, runs the Searches, reads each matched and named place, and records a Read Assessment of each area within them (`specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Check's Report § Read Assessment`). The builder designs from the assessments, stating a step's Objectives from them. So the builder's context holds what bears on the design, and none of the text that does not. Whatever else the builder reads, the canon it applies, a review's report or a finding's evidence, is its own reading, and sets nothing.

A place is assessed once, and its assessment stands. A place is first assessed when the builder researches it, or when a review's finding names a place no assessment covers, that place alone, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Settling a Round § The Round at Its Causes § Group` has it. An area already assessed is assessed again only where the user changes the goals or the frame in a way bearing on what was read, or where a review's finding shows the area's assessment wants a new perspective. The builder's brief then names the change or the finding, and the cold agent assesses again only the places it reaches. So a finding feeds an assessment, and an assessment the design, never the design an assessment. A step's revision and a round's settlement assess nothing again but on such a finding. The frame freezes at the plan's approval, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § The Adversarial Review § The Plan's Approval` has a change to the frame undo the approval. An assessment made after it reaches the design only as a finding the user settles, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Settling a Round § The Round at Its Causes § Group` has it.

### The Adversarial Review

When the builder judges the work optimal, a cold agent reads what the adversarial review's row of `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Checks What and Why` gives it, within the bound that section's opening sets, started with the standard brief alone (`specs/methodology/design-workflow.md § A Review's Brief`). The reviewer is never the builder, since re-reading your own work re-reads what you meant. It reviews as the persona of what the design changes (`specs/methodology/spec-placement.md § Personas`). The reviewer derives no solution of its own to the design's problem.

It judges the plan's structure first, by `specs/methodology/working-files.md § Design Plan Step § Step Objective § Semantic Checks [Name: objective-serves-its-goals]`, `specs/methodology/working-files.md § Design Plan Step § Step Objective § Semantic Checks [Name: objective-states-an-outcome]` and `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document § Frame Outcome § Semantic Checks [Name: outcome-upheld]`, asked of every step Objective and Frame Outcome. A round any of them fails reports those findings alone and judges the design no further. Its reviewer writes in its report's Title the field Stopped At, holding `the structure`, as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Check's Report` has a round stopped at the structure marked. A plan whose parts do not hold together fails whatever the rest of its design says, so a round reading the design's whole reach would spend itself on a design that must change first.

Past the structure, it confirms or denies that the design's steps bring its goals, in the context of its frame, to fruition, and that their Objectives let the implemented design be validated as accomplished, each Objective a state of the applied work that a reviewer can confirm or find unmet. The goals are its yardstick, the frame their context, and the steering decisions settled, not reopened. It looks hardest where the design decides, since that is where defects concentrate. It takes the design through the Design, Refactor, Refine process again, looking for Rube Goldberg machines and Potemkin villages (`specs/methodology/glossary.md`), and for a step patched where it should have been rewritten whole. It challenges whether the goals reach what the problem needs. A finding arguing that a goal misses it or reaches past it, or that the frame misses a place, is sorted as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Settling a Round § The Test` sorts it. What it would want different beyond its criteria, as its row states them, it reports as a review observation, as the opening of `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Checks What and Why` has it. It judges too whether each citation in the design resolves: to a heading in the files as they stand, to a heading a step adds, or to what the design states it relies on from a design its Depends On Designs names. While kept changes stand, it reads the files as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document § The States It Moves Through § A Send-back` has them read. It judges the design's Target against its Kind. It judges the frame's Assessed Areas, and the design against each whose Impact holds `applies`. It researches nothing: it redoes no sweep and assesses no area again. Where it holds that the frame misses a case, it raises a finding from its own reading, within the bound `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Checks What and Why` gives it. A passage within the Target that the change leaves false is its finding only where that passage holds a decision the steps do not make. One outside the Target is always its finding, the work's breach, lying in the plan as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document` bounds the apply, or, outside the frame, taking the route `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Settling a Round § The Test` gives a place outside the frame. It judges an implementation flaw by the frame and the canon. It edits nothing. It marks each finding a design flaw, an implementation flaw or a nit (`specs/methodology/glossary.md`), to be settled as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Converging, or Not` has it. It weighs each finding before reporting it, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Weighing a Review's Findings` has it. Each finding quotes its evidence from the files as they stand, and names what it bears on as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Check's Report § Finding` has a finding name it.

The builder confirms that each finding's quoted evidence stands in the files before acting on it, by searching for it, since a review can be confidently wrong. It settles an adversarial review's findings as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Settling a Round` has it. A round that finds a design flaw, as the user triages it, or an implementation flaw is followed by another round, which checks the fixes. A round stopped at the structure is followed by another round too, and never converges the review, whatever its findings settle to. The adversarial review converges at the first round not stopped at the structure that finds only nits the builder judges harmless, or nothing.

#### The Plan's Approval

The user's approval of the plan ends the review too. The user may give it in any state of the work, before any round or during one. The state in hand finishes its own work. An approval given in a reassessment is that reassessment's decision. A round still running or being settled is left with its findings as they are. The approval is logged as a `plan-approval` entry (`specs/methodology/working-files.md § Design Log Entry`). The design goes on to its validation and, once stamped, is approved. The approval authorizes the apply's writing, as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document` has it. It is applied where the user approved it with apply, once the apply may begin (`specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document § The States It Moves Through`). A change to the goals, the frame or a step after the approval undoes the approval. The design then comes back to the user to approve again, never to another round on its own, unless the user asks for one. After a review that converged instead, a change to the goals, the frame or a step takes another round (`specs/methodology/design-workflow.md § The Order of a Design's Work § The Work of a Design`). Then, with the user's go-ahead, the plan is applied, in the order `specs/methodology/design-workflow.md § The Order of a Design's Work § The Apply` gives: the builder writes the text or code, the post-apply review checks it, the user reviews it, and an audit runs.

### The Post-Apply Review

Once the builder has written the plan's text or code, a cold agent reads what the post-apply review's row of `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Checks What and Why` gives it, within the bound that section's opening sets, started with the standard brief alone, as the persona of what the design changes. It reads the applied work against the frozen design and asks, in order, whether the applied work meets each step's Objectives and changes nothing but the design's changes (`specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document`), whether the applied work achieves each Frame Outcome, by `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document § Frame Outcome § Semantic Checks [Name: outcome-upheld]`, whether the applied whole delivers the design goals, and whether its structure and wording hold, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Refactor` and `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Refine` judge a draft. It reads a change that a `direct-edit` entry logs as the builder's writing. It reads a change in a `user-edit` entry's places as the user's, never as a finding, and so too an Objective that an edit of the user's, kept by the user, leaves unmet. It reopens nothing the design decided. It marks each finding as the adversarial review marks its own, quoting its evidence and naming what it bears on as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Check's Report § Finding` has a finding name it, and says whether it lies in the plan or in the writing, or is on text whose migration the frame hands on, which lies in neither. It weighs each finding as the adversarial review weighs its own. A tactical fix (`specs/methodology/glossary.md`) lies in the writing, and so does an implementation flaw, a finding the writing settles by meeting a step's Objectives. A design fix (`specs/methodology/glossary.md`) lies in the plan, and so does any finding that would change the goals, the frame, the Target or a step's What or its Objectives. The builder settles each round as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Settling a Round` has it. Within the Target, it makes a tactical fix in the files. Outside the Target, it makes one as a direct edit the user approves, logged as a `direct-edit` entry naming its places. A finding outside the Target, and one on text whose migration the frame hands on, is settled as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document § Changes After Approval` has it. A finding lying in the plan goes to the user, whose triage sends the design back with its changes kept. The review converges at the first round that finds only nits, or nothing. The builder then shows the user the applied result, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § What the User Is Shown` has it. The user approves it, asks for another round, or gives feedback the builder takes into its writing, which the post-apply review then reads again.

### An Audit

An audit is a run of its skill, `.ai/skills/audit-specs/`'s, or for code `.ai/skills/verify-spec-implementation/`'s. It is the skill's full run, or, in a design's apply, the run the kinds of the files the design's Target names pick, its effort sized by the design's Kind (`specs/methodology/design-workflow.md § A Design's Reviews`), never narrowed to a design's changes. Run by the user, its report is the user's to read. Run by the builder in a design's apply, once the user approves the applied result, its report is the builder's, since the builder knows what it changed and why. The builder reads each finding against the diff between the files the apply altered, still uncommitted, and the last commit, which holds the files as they stood before the apply. Where the project has no version control, it reads against its own account of the files it changed. An audit names no design, so it cannot tell which change wrote what it reads. Where a rule reaches only what a change writes, the audit reports each breach of it wherever it finds one. The builder's triage tells what the design wrote from what it left as it stood. The builder triages each finding as caused by the design or not, and marks each it caused as the post-apply review marks its own: a design flaw, an implementation flaw or a nit, lying in the plan or in the writing, on text whose migration the frame hands on, or in a file the design's Kind does not let it change. It reads a change in a `user-edit` entry's places as the user's, never as a finding. Each is settled as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document § Changes After Approval` has it.

### Converging, or Not

A hard problem can take many review rounds, and each phase counts its own: the adversarial reviews of a design before it is approved, the post-apply reviews of its applied work, its audits, and its applies. A finding `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Settling a Round § The Test` keeps, whether an adversarial review's, a post-apply review's or an audit's, is a design flaw, an implementation flaw or a nit. A flaw is always named a design flaw or an implementation flaw.

#### A Design Flaw

Examples of a design flaw (`specs/methodology/glossary.md`) include a goal that misses what the problem needs or reaches past it, a place outside the frame that `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Settling a Round § The Test` makes a flaw in the frame, an approach that cannot reach a goal, and a Frame Outcome that does not judge one. The builder brings each design flaw to the user after the round, with the builder's recommended change to the design goals, the design frame or both. The user triages it, whoever uncovers it, the one writing the text or one reviewing it. Whether an item a review reports arguing the goals or the frame wrong is a design flaw is sorted as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Settling a Round § The Test` sorts what a review reports.

#### An Implementation Flaw

An implementation flaw (`specs/methodology/glossary.md`) can be of any seriousness. Examples include a case the scope names and the steps miss, a rule broken, and a file left out of sync. Before approval, the builder settles each and proves the fix by its self-review, and the reviewer's next round checks it. After the apply, a post-apply review's implementation flaw is settled as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § The Post-Apply Review` has it, and an audit's implementation flaw is settled as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document § Changes After Approval` has it.

#### A Nit

In an adversarial review, a nit (`specs/methodology/glossary.md`) is the builder's call. A round that finds a design flaw or an implementation flaw has all its nits fixed in the change the round makes. In a round that finds only nits, the builder judges each nit. Where the builder judges one harmful, because leaving it would harm the applied files or what is built from them, the builder marks it an implementation flaw. The round then has a flaw, and all its nits are fixed. Where the builder judges none harmful, it leaves them, and records each nit it leaves as a Settlement whose Verdict is nit in the round's report, as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Check's Report` declares it. A harmless nit is like lint on a moving blanket protecting an item in transit: the lint will not harm the item. Lint on the sterile cloth that holds a surgeon's scalpels is different, because it is not healthy for the patient. After the apply, a nit is a tactical fix, as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document § Changes After Approval` marks one. A post-apply review's nit is settled as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § The Post-Apply Review` settles a tactical fix, and an audit's nit as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document § Changes After Approval` has it.

#### The Runs

Every round of the adversarial review that the user's approval did not end, and every round of the post-apply review, is settled at its causes, whatever it finds, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Settling a Round § The Round at Its Causes` has it. The builder counts each run apart, and counts a round's design flaws by the user's triage:
- three adversarial rounds in a row that each found a design flaw mean the design is not converging, its goals or frame wrong past what its rounds can mend;
- five adversarial rounds in a row that each found an implementation flaw mean its steps are not converging;
- five post-apply rounds in a row that each found an implementation flaw lying in the writing mean its writing is not converging;
- three audits in a row that each sent a finding back to the writing mean its apply is not converging;
- three applies in a row that each ended with the design sent back mean the plan is not converging on what can be applied.

Any of these runs has the builder stop revising and take the design to the user, and the two step back and reassess. The builder brings its recommendation, with revised goals and frame where the run is of design flaws, and its case for why the work should now converge. They then rescope, split or abandon the design, or take a different approach, and start afresh with what was learned. Or the user has the work go on, and the run is not set back. Each further round that extends the run brings the builder and the user back to reassess, until a round ends the run.

### Each Review's Model and Effort

The adversarial review, the post-apply review and an audit in the apply each run on a model and at a reasoning effort (`specs/methodology/glossary.md`). The user chooses the model and the effort once for a design, as separate choices, before the design's first adversarial review. Where the user's approval ends the review before any round, the user chooses them before the design's post-apply review instead. The choices are kept for each later review and audit until the user changes them. The effort is light, medium or high. By default, a review runs on the model the agent runs on, at the effort the design's Kind gives it (`specs/methodology/design-workflow.md § A Design's Reviews`). A model other than the agent's own can see past a blind spot that a reviewer on the same model would share. A higher effort costs more. Neither changes what a review or an audit reads, nor a review's brief. A level is applied as `specs/methodology/design-workflow.md § A Review's Effort` has it. A review on a model the agent cannot start a reviewer on runs as the agent allows. The review's entry in the design's log names the model and the effort the review ran on, per `specs/methodology/working-files.md § Design Log Entry`.

### Direct Edits

A small tactical fix (`specs/methodology/glossary.md`), narrow in scope and low in risk, may skip this process and be applied directly, but only once the user has approved it. Made in a design's apply, it is logged as a `direct-edit` entry, one of the design's changes (`specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document`). Anything larger or riskier goes through the process. A working file is changed neither way, but as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § The Working Files` has it.

### A New Branch

The builder proposes a new branch's name and what the branch starts from. The branch is created only once the user approves it. How work is split across branches decides how the work is reviewed and merged, so the split is the user's to settle, as a direct edit is.

### Who Decides

A call the canon gives the user stays the user's, and is settled as its home sets. Such calls include a design flaw, a review observation, a post-apply review's finding lying in the plan, and how each audit finding `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Settling a Round § The Test` keeps is settled, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Converging, or Not` and `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document § Changes After Approval` have them. Every other call is the builder's, made on its own recommendation, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Converging, or Not` has it. An implementation flaw before approval is among the builder's calls. The builder asks a call of the user's that blocks the next step when the call arises. The calls that block nothing wait and go to the user with that blocking call, all together, each with the builder's recommendation, so the user answers in a word. The builder brings each review observation to the user with its round, as the round's settlement ends, or with its triage of an audit. An observation blocks none of the settlement. The round's settlement, or the audit's triage, records the user's answer to each call the builder brought.

### What the User Is Shown

The builder shows the user each thing the user approves with its summary, so what the user sees rests on no builder's choice:
- the plan, at its approval: what changed since the user last saw it and why, each change from the builder's self-review or from a review, how the builder settled the findings, and the findings each reviewer dropped with the reviewer's reasons;
- the applied result, at the user's review: what the applied work does, each round's counts by mark, the findings each reviewer dropped with the reviewer's reasons, how each finding was settled, and the checks the builder ran;
- the settled audit, before the commit: what the audit found and how each finding was settled, in the files or handed on.

### Who Checks What and Why

A check is feedback to whoever writes. The builder and the post-apply review run each check of the files or the code over the work, and the audit runs its checks over its units, never narrowed to the work, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § An Audit` has it. So the builder learns of an implementation flaw of its own before any review does. A compiler reports to an author the same way, on the code they wrote.

The reason serves the agent itself: a flaw is cheapest to fix where it is caused. An agent that sees no gain in a check takes the shortcut past it. The builder's self-review and the post-apply review read the reach of the work. The audit reads the audits and the files `specs/methodology/design-workflow.md § A Design's Reviews` sets for the kinds the Target names, never only what the design caused. The findings already standing in those files are among its findings, each triaged against the design's changes as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § An Audit` has an audit read, and one whose fix goes back to the writing runs that audit whole again, as `specs/methodology/design-workflow.md § The Order of a Design's Work § The Apply` routes it. So a flaw the work caused costs a reading of its reach where the work is checked, and far more once it reaches the audit. An agent skipping a check over its work to save time spends more than the check would have cost. Every review's cost and the audit's grow with the scope of the change, so the larger the change, the more early feedback saves. The design workflow orders the checks so the most immediate feedback on a rule is acted on before the flaw grows costlier.

The scope a check runs over a design's work is decided by its type alone, as `specs/methodology/checks.md § Check` declares the types. A script check reads the whole scope, as it stands and as it stood at the last commit. A semantic check reads the reach `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Measure Twice, Cut Once` has the builder zoom out to: around, up and across from each section the apply wrote in, or, in code or a script, from each unit it wrote. The candidates standing in that reach guide its reading and never bound it. What lies past that reach, the audit reads. Where a check runs, over a design or over its work, is stated as `specs/methodology/checks.md § Check` has it.

One test settles whose a finding of either kind is. A finding is the work's where the last commit, read by the rules as they stood in it, did not hold it: the diff wrote the breach, made text false that was true, or changed a rule that carried-over text now breaks. A semantic check tells which by reading its finding against the diff. A breach the last commit held by its own rules, carried over unchanged, is the audit's. A breach the apply rewrote and kept is the work's, since the diff wrote it. The last commit holds the files as they stood before the apply. Where the project has no version control, the test reads against the builder's own account of the files it changed, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § An Audit` has the builder read. A rule the apply changed may find a breach in text the apply carried over. The frame settles that breach, for a script check and a semantic check alike, as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document § Changes After Approval` settles it.

Until the checks' entries are records, the semantic checks over the work are those of the audit `specs/methodology/design-workflow.md § A Design's Reviews` sets for the kinds of the files the Target names: the rules of each reading audit that audit runs, in the audits table of `.ai/skills/audit-specs/SKILL.md`, and, where the kinds include `code`, the readings the table of checks of `.ai/skills/verify-spec-implementation/SKILL.md` lists.

The builder's self-review, the post-apply review and the audit each run in a state of the apply: the builder's self-review at `specs/methodology/design-workflow.md § The Order of a Design's Work § The Apply [States.State: writing]`, before the post-apply review; the post-apply review at `specs/methodology/design-workflow.md § The Order of a Design's Work § The Apply [States.State: post-apply reviewing]`; and the audit at `specs/methodology/design-workflow.md § The Order of a Design's Work § The Apply [States.State: auditing]`.

This table sets the checks a design goes through: who makes each check, what it reads, and what it leaves to another. A check asks only what its row gives, and no reviewer builds a script, a harness or a copy of the files to read anything. What a review would want different beyond its row's criteria it reports as a review observation (`specs/methodology/glossary.md`), apart from its review findings and uncounted, since the design bounds what is core to it.

No review, whether the adversarial review, the post-apply review or a handed-off judgment, reads a working file but the design it is given, its own report and each report the design's log entries name (`specs/methodology/working-files.md § Design Log Entry`). ### Reading the Earlier Reports

The reports a design's log entries name (`specs/methodology/working-files.md § Design Log Entry`) are read for what each check found and how its findings were settled. Where a report's settlement and the design differ, the design governs.

### Weighing a Review's Findings

The adversarial review and the post-apply review each weigh their findings before writing the report, in a collecting pass and a weighing pass.

In the collecting pass, the reviewer collects every issue it finds, marking each finding and setting each review observation apart as its row of `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Checks What and Why` has it.

In the weighing pass, the reviewer weighs each finding by `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Settling a Round § The Test`. A finding it keeps is reported with its rationale and a possible fix. The rationale shows what goes wrong in the work if the finding is left. A finding landing on text an earlier round's change added names that round in its rationale. Where the finding's fix is a tactical fix (`specs/methodology/glossary.md`), the possible fix is that tactical fix. Otherwise the possible fix names the decision the finding touches and what that decision's revision must deliver. The reviewer leaves the revision to the builder, which makes it at the round's causes, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Settling a Round § The Round at Its Causes § Propose` has it. A finding it drops is reported as dropped, with the reason the reviewer changed its mind. A review observation is reported with the reviewer's reason for judging the work wrong, and with no fix.

Between its passes, the reviewer reads the reports the design's log entries name, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Reading the Earlier Reports` has them read. A finding the builder dropped in an earlier round is raised again only with an argument that round's settlement did not weigh.

A reviewer's dropped findings, with its reasons, show where its first reading was preference.

### Settling a Round

#### The Test

The builder addresses a review finding only where this test needs it addressed, or where the user asked for it. Each review's criteria, as its row of `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Checks What and Why` states them, hold the design to its goals, its frame and its steps. They hold it to the canon's rules over the design's own composition alone, as the design's goals and frame may change those rules. So a breach in text the design only reads bears on no review's criteria. Each item a review reports, and each finding of an audit or of the builder's self-review this test is applied to, is anchored in the plan, the work's breach, or neither:
- **Anchored in the plan.** It bears on a stated goal, Frame Outcome or step Objective inside the frame.
- **The work's breach.** It shows the design's own composition breaking the canon. Before the apply, that is text the design writes or its steps would leave wrong. After it, that is a breach the opening of `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Checks What and Why` makes the work's. It is needed wherever its text lies, a place outside the frame taking the route this section gives one.
- **Neither.** It is a review observation (`specs/methodology/glossary.md`), or a call `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Decides` gives the user, a breach in text the design only reads among them.

A finding showing the work's breach is always needed. A finding anchored in the plan is needed only where it shows what goes wrong in the work if it is left, and what goes wrong is one of:
- the goals or the frame are wrong, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Converging, or Not § A Design Flaw` has a design flaw;
- the work contradicts itself, its goals or the canon;
- an Objective cannot be confirmed;
- the case it names is one a goal's or an Objective's own words name, and the work misses it;
- a reference or a value is wrong.

A finding arguing that a goal misses what the problem needs, or reaches past it, is a design flaw. The findings an audit makes that the design did not cause are no review's. The test drops none of them, and each goes to the user by the first row of `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document § Changes After Approval`.

One rule routes a place outside the frame, whether the builder's research or a review's finding brings it. A place outside the frame that the change leaves wrong is handed on where the design already hands its work on, as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Check's Report § Where a Finding Lies` has a finding handed on. Any other place the change leaves wrong is a flaw in the frame, and so is a case the change leaves right that a stated goal's own words reach. The builder brings such a flaw to the user, as a design flaw. Any other place outside the frame is nothing to the design, and an argument that the frame take it in is a review observation, brought to the user as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Decides` has one brought.

A finding is bloat where it asks for mechanism, such as a state, a field, a flag, a rule or a check, to cover a case that no goal's or Objective's words name, or a risk that a guard already in the canon or the design covers. It is dropped, or, where it may merit a change of its own, brought to the user as a review observation. Every other finding the test does not need is dropped too. A dropped finding holds no mark, so no count by mark, no Fact and no run reads it. It is listed as dropped in the round's settlement, or in the builder's triage of an audit, and counted in Dropped (`specs/methodology/working-files.md § Design Log Entry`). A design flaw, a finding that the goals or the frame are wrong, is never dropped by the builder and goes to the user, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Decides` has it. The builder sorts each item a review reports by what it bears on, never by the label the reviewer gave it. One anchored in the plan or showing the work's breach is a review finding, settled by the test. One that this section makes a design flaw is a design flaw. Any other is a review observation, or a call `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Decides` gives the user.

The builder weighs each finding the reviewer kept and each it dropped, taking in the reviewer's rationale and its possible fix. Where the builder's verdict on a finding differs from the reviewer's, or the round's change settles less than the reviewer's possible fix reaches, the round's settlement says why.

#### The Round at Its Causes

The builder settles every finding the test keeps within the design goals and the design frame, bringing the user, with its recommendation, only each call that `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Decides` gives the user, among them a finding it cannot settle within the goals and frame. A reviewer's findings are what the reviewer saw of the design. They are often right, and often symptoms of a cause that the reviewer, not being the builder, does not name. A finding fixed where it sits can leave its cause to produce the next round's findings. So the builder settles each round of an adversarial review that the user's approval did not end, and each round of a post-apply review, as design work, never one finding at a time. The builder first confirms each finding's quoted evidence, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § The Adversarial Review` has it. An audit's findings are settled as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document § Changes After Approval` has them. To settle a round, the builder steps back.

##### Group

Before grouping, the builder reads the earlier reports, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Reading the Earlier Reports` has them read. It hands to a cold agent each place a finding of the round names that no Read Assessment covers, for a first assessment, and each area a finding shows wants a new perspective, for an assessment again, the brief naming the finding, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Measure Twice, Cut Once` has research done. Before the plan's approval, it takes those assessments into the frame, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § The Goals and the Frame` has an assessed area taken in. Only then does it find the round's causes, from the findings, the assessments and the earlier reports, so it judges a flaw's scope from what the places a finding names hold rather than from the line it quotes. It confirms that a finding's quoted evidence stands by searching for it. In an adversarial round, it reads no place a finding names.

In a post-apply round, the frame stands frozen. A finding naming a new place whose Impact holds `changes` is settled as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document § Changes After Approval` settles a finding outside the Target, and goes to the user. A finding naming a new place whose Impact holds `applies` alone is fixed following that place. The builder reads fresh, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Reading Fresh` has a file read, what the round's change rewrites and what that writing follows, a new place among it wherever its assessment is other than `none`. It records that reading in the round's Settling Reads, as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Check's Report` has it.

It groups the round's findings by the decision of the design each touches, such as a rule the design states, a form it chooses, a unit it writes or its frame's reach. The groups take in the findings of the phase's earlier rounds too, and those a judgment the builder handed off has made since the last round. No finding is fixed as it arrives. Of each decision drawing findings, the builder asks what lets them in. It asks first of each decision drawing findings again, one an earlier round of the phase drew findings on too. Of such a decision, before any other cause, it asks whether the goals and the frame are the cause, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § The Goals and the Frame § Whether the Goals and the Frame Name a Mechanism` has it. That test asks only whether the goals and the frame name the mechanism. Where it is not asked, or finds that they name it, the cause may still be another design flaw in the goals or in the frame, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Converging, or Not § A Design Flaw` names them. It may be an implementation flaw instead, such as a case the scope names and the steps miss, a rule stated in pieces with no one home, or a form chosen wrong.

Of each finding the test keeps that is marked a design flaw or an implementation flaw, the builder then asks why its own Design, Refactor, Refine pass and self-review let the flaw through. The answer is the flaw's miss (`specs/methodology/glossary.md`). Before it proposes the round's change, the builder asks each question a miss names of the work, recording the answer the work gives it beside the miss. In an adversarial round, it answers from the assessments. In a post-apply round, it first reads each place a miss names, fresh as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Reading Fresh` has a file read.

##### Weigh by the Goals

The builder weighs against the design's goals each finding that asks the design to do more, less or other, and each change it would propose, the test dropping what the work does not need. The builder settles an implementation flaw in the round's change.

##### Weigh the Steers

The builder weighs the steers that the causes press on, as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § Steering Decision § Revisiting a Steer` has it.

##### Propose

The builder states the round's causes, each flaw's miss beside its cause, and the one change it proposes at them, with each finding's settlement under it. A cause holding only nits is settled as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Converging, or Not § A Nit` has nits settled. In a post-apply round, a cause lying in the plan goes to the user, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § The Post-Apply Review` places a finding. Of the other causes, one whose fix is a tactical fix (`specs/methodology/glossary.md`) has that fix made at every place its defect stands. The settlement gives the reason its fix is a tactical one. Every other cause has the decision it lies in revised whole, by Design, Refactor, Refine from that decision's problem, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Design` starts a draft. The revision is written whole, never as a clause wedged into the decision as it stands, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Measure Twice, Cut Once` has a fix written. Each clause wedged in opens a path the next round finds, so a revision made now costs less than the rounds a patch would bring. Where the goals or the frame let findings in, their revision comes first. A round goes to the user when it holds a call that `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Decides` gives the user, a design flaw or a steer the round's change presses on among them. The user triages the round's design flaws and answers its other calls, approving revised goals or a revised frame. The builder settles the round's implementation flaws and nits as on any round. Any other round the builder settles itself.

The round's change is the least of the revisions at its causes, weighed by what the revised work holds. It is the revision delivering the goals with the fewest rules, as `specs/methodology/glossary.md` has elegance, and with the least text. It is never weighed by how little of the decision as it stands it changes, nor by the text each finding needs. A change removing text, or citing the home that holds the fact, comes before one adding text. The change writes what it reaches whole, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Measure Twice, Cut Once` has a fix written. Where a finding lands on text that an earlier round's change added, the builder first weighs again, by `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Settling a Round § The Test`, the finding that change settled. Where that finding is not needed, the least change removes what the earlier change added, which settles both findings. A steer the earlier change carried out is weighed as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § Steering Decision § Revisiting a Steer` has it.

##### Change and Prove

The builder revises the design as the round was settled, top-down, as `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Measure Twice, Cut Once` has a change made, or, in a post-apply round, the files. It then proves the round's change by its self-review, as its row of `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Checks What and Why` sets, never by rereading the round's change alone. It proves it before the next round, or, where no round follows, before the design is validated or, after a post-apply round, before the user's review.

#### After the Last Round

When the builder hands off a judgment after the phase's last round, the judgment's findings are settled together, in one pass, before the design is validated or, after the post-apply review, before the user's review. Where a round completes any run that `§ Design, Refactor, Refine § Converging, or Not § The Runs` sets, the design is not revised until the builder and the user have reassessed it, as that section has it.
