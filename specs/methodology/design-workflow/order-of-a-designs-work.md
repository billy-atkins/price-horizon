## The Order of a Design's Work

Two State Machines set the order a design's work takes, and what moves it back. The design's Status runs them ([`specs/methodology/working-files/main.md § A Design Document § The States It Moves Through`](/specs/methodology/working-files/main.md#the-states-it-moves-through)). [`§ The Order of a Design's Work § The Work of a Design`](#the-work-of-a-design) governs the in-progress state, and [`§ The Order of a Design's Work § The Apply`](#the-apply) governs the applying state. Each state's work is done as the section it cites has it, so the machines hold the order alone. A state running a check of a design names it as [`specs/methodology/checks.md § Check`](/specs/methodology/checks.md#check) has a check named where it runs. The principles stay in [`specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine`](/specs/AGENTS.md#design-refactor-refine). A design changing code takes the same order ([`specs/methodology/working-files/main.md § A Design Document § A Design Changing Code`](/specs/methodology/working-files/main.md#a-design-changing-code)).

Before any other act on a design, whatever its Status, its builder runs the design script's check of the design's own names and, where its Depends On Designs names a finished or gone design, frees the design, as [`specs/methodology/working-files/main.md § A Design Document § What Blocks What`](/specs/methodology/working-files/main.md#what-blocks-what) has a name read: before a design not yet started is taken up, before a move of an approved or applying design's Status, and before any work on one in progress. A design freed where goals agreed? holds is reassessed and takes the transition its state has on the design being freed, its work beginning at framing afresh. One freed where goals agreed? does not hold takes no transition and goes on where it stood.

### The Work of a Design

A design enters its work by the reason its Status entered in-progress. A plan stands where framing agreed? and steps drafted? both hold ([`§ The Order of a Design's Work § Facts`](#facts)). The design's change leads back to another adversarial review wherever needs review? holds or framing agreed? does not, unless the user's approval has ended its review.
