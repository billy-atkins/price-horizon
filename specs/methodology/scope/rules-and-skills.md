## Rules and Skills

The methodology's files state Spec of Record's methodology rules. They do not sequence the work, record what goes wrong while following it, check whether a result conforms, or teach the craft a rule assumes. The skills that [`specs/methodology/skills/registered-skills.md § Registered Skills`](/specs/methodology/skills/registered-skills.md#registered-skills) registers do those things, and a cold agent needs them to follow the methodology in practice. [`specs/AGENTS.md`](/specs/AGENTS.md) states methodology rules too, and sequences the work through the Design, Refactor, Refine process. The methodology's files and [`specs/AGENTS.md`](/specs/AGENTS.md), with the specifications' main file and their overview, make up the core canon ([`specs/methodology/glossary/glossary.md`](/specs/methodology/glossary/glossary.md)). The skills, with the code they share, are the rest of the canon. No skill is a methodology rule's home. Where a skill and a methodology rule disagree, the methodology rule governs, and the skill is what needs correcting.

A rule without a check is a suggestion, so every rule of Spec of Record is enforced. A rule added or changed takes its check with it, in the same change. A check of the files or the code is run where [`specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Checks What and Why § The Scope a Check Runs Over`](/specs/AGENTS.md#the-scope-a-check-runs-over) runs it, over the work as well as over the audit's units. [`specs/methodology/spec-style/clear-prose.md § Clear Prose`](/specs/methodology/spec-style/clear-prose.md#clear-prose), as it reaches the working files, stands without a check. The user holds the working files to it in reading them, since an audit of files that never last would cost more than it finds. [`specs/methodology/spec-style/markdown.md § Blank Lines`](/specs/methodology/spec-style/markdown.md#blank-lines), and the layout [`specs/methodology/modeling-constructs.md § Fields`](/specs/methodology/modeling-constructs.md#fields) gives a block of fields, stand without a check where they reach the skills' own output that no design's log entry names, or a plan no validation reads: a design once it is complete or abandoned, and any plan other than a design document. Each such file is read by the work it was written for, so an audit of them would cost more than it finds. A check's report a design's log entry names is held to them, since the skill writing the design reads its records by that layout and checks it as it appends the check's entry ([`specs/methodology/working-files/main.md § A Check's Report § Script Checks [Name: report-laid-out]`](/specs/methodology/working-files/main.md#script-checks-1)).

**A specification rule.** It is checked against the files it governs by one of the audit skill's reading audits. A rule of [`specs/methodology/code.md`](/specs/methodology/code.md) is checked instead by a check of `.ai/skills/verify-spec-implementation/` that reads the code. Either skill's script first decides whatever the files alone settle. The script never decides a judgment, since a check that cannot decide gives a confident false clear. A script that lists places for a reading audit to look decides nothing.

**An operating rule.** It is checked where it leaves a trace in the files the audits read: the scope's tree ([`specs/methodology/scope/what-spec-of-record-governs.md § The Shape of the Scope`](/specs/methodology/scope/what-spec-of-record-governs.md#the-shape-of-the-scope)), the skills registry ([`specs/methodology/skills/registered-skills.md § Registered Skills`](/specs/methodology/skills/registered-skills.md#registered-skills)), a skill's form ([`specs/methodology/skills/authoring-a-skill.md § Authoring a Skill`](/specs/methodology/skills/authoring-a-skill.md#authoring-a-skill)), a follow-up. A plan's form leaves its trace outside those files. The skill that writes the plan checks the plan's form, through the skill's script and steps. A rule whose step records its result in a check's report leaves its trace in that report ([`specs/methodology/working-files/main.md § A Check's Report`](/specs/methodology/working-files/main.md#a-checks-report)). The skill writing the design checks it there through its script as it appends the check's entry, as it checks a plan's form through its script. Where a rule leaves no trace, as reading fresh leaves none, since no record shows when a place was read, the rule is carried out as a named step of [`specs/AGENTS.md`](/specs/AGENTS.md) or of a registered skill. A skipped step is found by what it leaves wrong in the files.

**Which check enforces each section.** `.ai/skills/audit-specs/` covers every section of [`specs/AGENTS.md`](/specs/AGENTS.md), of the main file of `specs/` and of the methodology's files, except the methodology's `architecture.md` and `index.md`, its worked examples under `examples/`, each form template, told by its name ([`specs/methodology/scope/form-templates.md § Form Templates`](/specs/methodology/scope/form-templates.md#form-templates)), and each record section of `Script Checks` or `Semantic Checks`, which hold no rule of their own. For each section, it names the reading audit that enforces the section, or names the section as one stating operating rules, in a Skipped Step's Sections ([`§ Rules and Skills § Skipped Step`](#skipped-step)). A check's record standing directly under a section names that section too ([`specs/methodology/checks.md § Check`](/specs/methodology/checks.md#check)). It names no section below that one. An operating rule's check is a step under a registered skill's `## Workflow` that applies the rule. That step gives the rule a point-of-use citation. For a section of [`specs/methodology/code.md`](/specs/methodology/code.md), `.ai/skills/verify-spec-implementation/` names the check that enforces it instead, or `.ai/skills/audit-specs/` names it a section stating operating rules.

**A section read in parts.** An audit that reads only part of a section it names says which part. The audits that name one section read all of it between them, each part once. So the checks whose records stand directly under a section enforce all of it between them, each of its rules held to by at least one. Until a section's checks move into records, the entries of the skills' tables and the audits naming the section count among the checks standing under it. A Skipped Step is no check: naming the section in its Sections names it as one stating operating rules, whose check is the step carrying them out. An entry in a skill's table, or a Skipped Step, covers the section it names and the sections under it. Once the entry moves into records, each of those sections is covered by the records standing directly under it. Where a step names the section too, the step carries what leaves no trace in the files, and the audits read the rest.

### Skipped Step

A Skipped Step is one failure a step carrying out an operating rule leaves when it is skipped: the sections stating the operating rules the step carries out, the rule the failure breaks, what it leaves in the files, and what catches it. It is a record type ([`specs/methodology/modeling-constructs.md § Constructs § Record Form`](/specs/methodology/modeling-constructs.md#record-form)), its records standing in a record section of `.ai/skills/audit-specs/`:

**Construct:** `specs/methodology/modeling-constructs.md § Constructs § Record Form` \
**Plural Name:** Skipped Steps \
**Reference Key:** Failure

| Field | Unique | Required | Default Value | Values | Holds |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Failure | yes | yes | | text | the failure, in a few words |
| Sections | no | yes | | text | a list, each item a citation of a section stating the operating rules the failure skips |
| Rule | no | yes | | text | a citation of the place stating the rule the failure breaks |
| Leaves | no | yes | | text | what the failure leaves in the files |
| Caught By | no | yes | | text | a list, an item to each check catching the failure: a citation of a place; a skill's directory, then its script's flag or the step of the skill, as Carried Out By names a skill's check ([`specs/methodology/checks.md § Check`](/specs/methodology/checks.md#check)); a citation of a Script Check record; or the user, where the user is its check |
