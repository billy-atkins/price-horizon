# Spec of Record: Working Files

**Reference Key:** File \
**Required:** Description

| File | Description |
|---|---|
| `main.md` | The working files, and how the builder changes one, never by a design. The form of a design document, the plan for its change, and of a follow-up, with the states each moves through. A design's Frame Outcomes, its Assessed Areas holding its current scope, and its steps, each naming the steps it builds on, stating its Step Objectives as outcomes and carrying any Draft of its means. The folder a design's files sit in. The kinds of file a design is bounded to, and how a design changing code differs. How a design spawns a change of its own, what blocks what, what a design relies on from the designs it depends on, and what their finishing does to it. A design's version, in Semantic Versioning's form, worked out from its log, and the reports of its current major version that bind it. A design's log of each check and event of its work, and what the log is for, and its counts taken from each check's report. The form of that report, which holds the builder's two indicators of each finding it keeps, what requires it and on whose authority it is fixed or left, and the order and lineage of each finding and each change in a line of fixes, and the builder's work in the check: what it read, searched, traced and asked, with a cold agent's Read Assessments where the check is research. What the design script checks that report against. The wording a design retires, and what a design's changes are. The record's checks a validation review makes of a design document's structure, reading beside it the reports of the handed-off judgments of its current major version that its log names. How a finding after the approval is settled: a tactical fix made in place and proved by the builder's passes, and every other fix sending the design back. What becomes of each uncommitted change at each event of a design's work, and how a design sent back keeps its changes while another applies. A design document's goals and steering decisions: each steer a selection or a perspective, told apart from direction, feedback on the applied result, a decision about other work and the user's own edit during an apply or while the design is sent back, and revisited where a review round's cause presses on one. A stamp, when a design's own working files take a form it changes, and how a design changing the process runs under the process it writes |
| `check-report/` | The form template of a check's report for each kind of report, each part and field citing the rule or record type defining it and the rule naming the hand that writes it. See `check-report/index.md`. |
