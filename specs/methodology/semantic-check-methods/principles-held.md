## Principles Held

This check reads the files against the constitutional principles. The reader makes the semantic checks that stand under [`specs/AGENTS.md § Constitutional Principles`](/specs/AGENTS.md#constitutional-principles) and under each principle.

What is easy to miss:
- a unit that was amended and never written again: a clause or a case forced in beside text left as it was, where each sentence reads true on its own. [`specs/AGENTS.md § Constitutional Principles § Revise, Never Patch`](/specs/AGENTS.md#revise-never-patch) says what a patch is. A reader with no change to compare against cannot see what a change replaced, and reads the unit as it stands;
- a rule below the principles that relaxes one by an exception for its own case and names no principle.
