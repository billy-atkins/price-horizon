## How the Guarantees Are Achieved Mechanically

The product specification states the guarantees this system makes. Here is how each one is built.

**A repeatable answer.** An answer is repeatable through deterministic tool calls rather than LLM freehand math, versioned models and weighting rubrics, and question-intent caching. Reuse only a context-independent parsing template for an identical normalized utterance, language and compatible parser and glossary revisions. For each request, bind the unit, entities, geography, horizon, target period, metric, granularity and decision context afresh under current authorization. Reuse of a forecast follows the separate published-bundle selector. Numeric results repeat for the same authorized intent, decision, data, application and model versions, business-unit settings and original forecast scoring date. Retrieving a published or stored forecast preserves its confidence assessment. A new scheduled or live calculation uses its own UTC date and may assign older evidence a different freshness score. An answer is reproducible against an Answer Provenance ([`specs/application/technical/data-model.md § Core Data Model § Pipeline and Answer Artifacts § Answer Provenance`](/specs/application/technical/data-model.md#answer-provenance)) logged alongside it. For a live-path answer, the Answer Provenance is persisted on the Delivered Answer record produced for Position and Simulate ([`specs/application/technical/data-model.md § Core Data Model § Pipeline and Answer Artifacts § Delivered Answer`](/specs/application/technical/data-model.md#delivered-answer)), and for Predict on the Effective Price Forecast row's own pinned fields ([`specs/application/technical/data-model.md § Core Data Model § Pipeline and Answer Artifacts § Effective Price Forecast`](/specs/application/technical/data-model.md#effective-price-forecast)). The Effective Price Forecast row already serves this role for a grid-served answer too. The application and the models version independently ([`specs/application/technical/engineering-and-production-considerations.md § Engineering and Production Considerations`](/specs/application/technical/engineering-and-production-considerations.md#engineering-and-production-considerations)). An upgrade to one without the other is the kind of change this record is built to make visible rather than silent. That record covers every structured fact in the answer. It does not cover the rendered narrative's wording, which [`specs/application/technical/query-service/layered-output-synthesis.md § Layered Output Synthesis (producing the answer) § Narrative rendering`](/specs/application/technical/query-service/layered-output-synthesis.md#narrative-rendering) holds to a looser standard. Reproducibility holds for the positioning and simulation engines ([`specs/application/technical/query-service/layered-output-synthesis.md § Layered Output Synthesis (producing the answer)`](/specs/application/technical/query-service/layered-output-synthesis.md#layered-output-synthesis-producing-the-answer)) too. With the same origin prices, resolved matches, Predict output and decision type, the same candidate rule produces the same eligible seeds and prices. The same outcomes, stage availability and pinned materiality settings produce the same ordered groups. A historical answer reads its saved labels, membership and wording. The same option against the same models produces the same simulated outcome. The golden-question harness ([`specs/application/technical/evaluation-and-monitoring.md § Evaluation and monitoring`](/specs/application/technical/evaluation-and-monitoring.md#evaluation-and-monitoring)) runs in CI whenever a model, prompt template, or data pipeline version changes, to catch drift before it reaches a user. The batch and live paths run on the terms [`specs/application/technical/materialization-service/forecast-materialization.md § Forecast materialization`](/specs/application/technical/materialization-service/forecast-materialization.md#forecast-materialization) sets. The forecast identifies its scheduled or live origin and target window. Identical pinned inputs yield identical numeric results.

**An answer enriched by quantitative data.** Each displayed figure identifies the evidence and computation that produced it. [`specs/application/technical/model-registry.md § Model registry`](/specs/application/technical/model-registry.md#model-registry) assigns the Position archetype and grouping rules to the application release. Forecast and simulation evidence identifies the registered tools actually invoked and their versions. The scorecard, map, positioning options, simulated outcomes and their narrative can all be traced to these records.

**An answer grounded in qualitative information.** Through the vector store and the business glossary, qualitative context is retrieved, weighed into the forecast, and checked against the baseline during driver attribution. It is not appended afterward as commentary.

**An answer that shows how it was built.** By construction, the forecast's move is the baseline move plus the drivers' contributions ([`specs/application/technical/predict-computation.md § Predict Computation § Driver Attribution`](/specs/application/technical/predict-computation.md#driver-attribution)). For a forecast, the Rationale Record ([`specs/application/technical/data-model.md § Core Data Model § Pipeline and Answer Artifacts § Rationale Record`](/specs/application/technical/data-model.md#rationale-record)) carries every term of that sum and the evidence behind each term. At every stage, the Rationale Record also carries what set each confidence component. It is written for Predict and Position by [`specs/application/technical/predict-computation.md § Predict Computation § Confidence and rationale package`](/specs/application/technical/predict-computation.md#confidence-and-rationale-package), and for a Simulated Outcome by [`specs/application/technical/query-service/layered-output-synthesis.md § Layered Output Synthesis (producing the answer) § Confidence and rationale package, extended`](/specs/application/technical/query-service/layered-output-synthesis.md#confidence-and-rationale-package-extended). A below-grid result identifies its grounded parent and any local inputs or weights used ([`specs/application/technical/query-service/layered-output-synthesis.md § Layered Output Synthesis (producing the answer) § Drill-down mechanics`](/specs/application/technical/query-service/layered-output-synthesis.md#drill-down-mechanics)).

**Guardrail underneath every guarantee, mechanically.** [`specs/application/product/trust-and-explainability/guarantees.md § Guarantees This Answer Engine Makes`](/specs/application/product/trust-and-explainability/guarantees.md#guarantees-this-answer-engine-makes) states the guarantee that the system flags uncertainty rather than guessing. That guarantee is enforced by:
- the retained forecast warning from [`specs/application/technical/predict-computation.md § Predict Computation § Confidence and rationale package § Forecast confidence warning`](/specs/application/technical/predict-computation.md#forecast-confidence-warning), displayed with the figures independently of narrative generation;
- the glossary resolution check in intent parsing ([`specs/application/technical/query-service/intent-and-retrieval.md § Dynamic Retrieval and Weighting (when the question is asked) § Intent parsing and decoupling`](/specs/application/technical/query-service/intent-and-retrieval.md#intent-parsing-and-decoupling)), which stops the pipeline and asks for clarification rather than guessing an unresolved entity or an unsettled kind of decision;
- the human review queue, through a Review Queue Item ([`specs/application/technical/data-model.md § Core Data Model § Pipeline and Answer Artifacts § Review Queue Item`](/specs/application/technical/data-model.md#review-queue-item)), for a low-confidence product match, an unresolved source geography, or an ambiguous or incomplete document extraction. Figures that depend on the flagged evidence remain unavailable pending resolution.
