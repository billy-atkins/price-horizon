## How the Guarantees Are Achieved Mechanically

The product specification states the guarantees this system makes. Here is how each is actually built.

**A repeatable answer —** deterministic tool calls rather than LLM freehand math, versioned models and weighting rubrics, and question-intent caching keyed on entities, the business unit and kind of decision the question is answered for, plus data-as-of version, so an unchanged question returns the same answer every time as long as the data, application version, model versions, business unit configuration version, and kind of decision behind it are all unchanged too. Concretely, an answer is reproducible against an Answer Provenance (`specs/application/technical/data-model.md § Core Data Model § Pipeline and Answer Artifacts § Answer Provenance`) logged alongside it, actually persisted, for a live-path answer, on the Delivered Answer record produced for Position and Simulate (`specs/application/technical/data-model.md § Core Data Model § Pipeline and Answer Artifacts § Delivered Answer`), and, for Predict, on the Effective Price Forecast row's own pinned fields (`specs/application/technical/data-model.md § Core Data Model § Pipeline and Answer Artifacts § Effective Price Forecast`), which already serves this role for a grid-served answer too. Since application and models now version independently (`specs/application/technical/engineering-and-production-considerations.md § Engineering and Production Considerations`), an upgrade to one without the other is exactly the kind of change this record is built to make visible rather than silent. That record covers every structured fact in the answer, not the rendered narrative's wording, which `specs/application/technical/query-service/layered-output-synthesis.md § Layered Output Synthesis (producing the answer) § Narrative rendering` holds to a looser standard. Reproducibility holds for the positioning and simulation engines (`specs/application/technical/query-service/layered-output-synthesis.md § Layered Output Synthesis (producing the answer)`) too: the same candidate generation logic against the same Predict output and the same decision type produces the same option set, and the same option against the same models produces the same simulated outcome. The golden-question harness (`specs/application/technical/evaluation-and-monitoring.md § Evaluation and monitoring`) runs in CI whenever a model, prompt template, or data pipeline version changes, to catch drift before it reaches a user. The batch and live paths run on the terms `specs/application/technical/materialization-service/forecast-materialization.md § Forecast materialization` sets, so which path answered a question is never something a user could detect from the answer itself.

**An answer enriched by quantitative data —** every layer of the output traces back to a named model or dataset in the registry, including the positioning and simulation models introduced in `specs/application/technical/query-service/layered-output-synthesis.md § Layered Output Synthesis (producing the answer)`. Nothing in the scorecard, the map, the options frame, the outcome comparison, or the narrative is asserted without a retrievable, versioned source behind it.

**An answer grounded in qualitative information —** the vector store and business glossary mean qualitative context is retrieved, and weighed into the forecast and checked against the baseline during driver attribution, not appended afterward as commentary.

**An answer that shows how it was built —** the forecast's move is the baseline move plus the drivers' contributions by construction (`specs/application/technical/predict-computation.md § Predict Computation § Driver Attribution`), and the Rationale Record (`specs/application/technical/data-model.md § Core Data Model § Pipeline and Answer Artifacts § Rationale Record`) carries, for a forecast, every term of that sum and the evidence behind each, and, at every stage, what set each confidence component. It is written for Predict and Position by `specs/application/technical/predict-computation.md § Predict Computation § Confidence and rationale packaging`, and for a Simulated Outcome by `specs/application/technical/query-service/layered-output-synthesis.md § Layered Output Synthesis (producing the answer) § Confidence and rationale packaging, extended`. An allocated figure below the grid names the figure it was apportioned from and the weighting used (`specs/application/technical/query-service/layered-output-synthesis.md § Layered Output Synthesis (producing the answer) § Drill-down mechanics`).

**Guardrail underneath every guarantee, mechanically —** the guarantee stated in `specs/application/product/trust-and-explainability/guarantees.md § Guarantees This Answer Engine Makes`, that the system flags uncertainty rather than guessing, is enforced by the confidence_score and confidence_components fields attached to every Rationale Record (`specs/application/technical/data-model.md § Core Data Model § Pipeline and Answer Artifacts § Rationale Record`), by the glossary resolution check in intent parsing (`specs/application/technical/query-service/intent-and-retrieval.md § Dynamic Retrieval and Weighting (when the question is asked) § Intent parsing and decoupling`) that stops the pipeline and asks for clarification rather than guessing an unresolved entity or an unsettled kind of decision, and by the human review queue, a Review Queue Item (`specs/application/technical/data-model.md § Core Data Model § Pipeline and Answer Artifacts § Review Queue Item, a Lifecycle`), for the low-confidence product match, ambiguous document extraction, or incomplete extraction flagged upstream, before any of them ever reaches an answer.
