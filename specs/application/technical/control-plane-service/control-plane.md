## Control Plane

A named capability, not a configuration file, the client-facing surface (`specs/application/product/tenant-administration/reference-data-management.md`, `specs/application/product/tenant-administration/business-unit-settings.md`, `specs/application/product/tenant-administration/data-source-connections.md`, `specs/application/product/tenant-administration/review-queues.md`, `specs/application/product/tenant-administration/audit-log-visibility.md`, `specs/application/product/tenant-administration/application-log-control.md`) for managing everything in `specs/application/technical/data-model.md § Core Data Model` that varies by business unit without needing Acme AI's involvement for routine changes. The line follows the same one drawn in `specs/application/technical/engineering-and-production-considerations.md § Engineering and Production Considerations`, mechanism stays canonical, tuning is configuration, stated here as a Constraint per config surface.

The control plane service also serves read-only views of installation-wide values. The application version the installation is pinned to and each model's own pinned version (`specs/application/technical/engineering-and-production-considerations.md § Engineering and Production Considerations`), the views `specs/application/product/platform-and-compliance-operations/versioned-upgrades.md § Versioned Upgrades` promises, are read by a Control plane admin on this surface and by a Platform admin through Acme AI's own tooling. The audit log's retention_period currently in effect, the view `specs/application/product/tenant-administration/audit-log-visibility.md § Audit Log Visibility` promises, is read by a Control plane admin on this surface. The kinds of model call the installation's configuration lets reach a frontier model provider (`specs/application/technical/model-registry.md § Model registry`), the view `specs/application/product/platform-and-compliance-operations/deployment-topology.md § Deployment Topology § Frontier Models` promises, is read by a Control plane admin on this surface too. Any Control plane admin claim reads the same installation-wide value, whichever business unit it is scoped to, and no view can change what it shows. A Platform admin's read is Acme AI access through PriceHorizon's systems, recorded in the audit log like any other (`specs/application/technical/engineering-and-production-considerations.md § Engineering and Production Considerations § Security and governance`); a Control plane admin's is not.

`specs/application/technical/architecture.md § Architecture Overview § Diagrams § Layer Composition` renders this.
