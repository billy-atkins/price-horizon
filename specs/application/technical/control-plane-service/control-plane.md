## Control Plane

A named capability, not a configuration file, the client-facing surface (`specs/application/product/tenant-administration/reference-data-management.md`, `specs/application/product/tenant-administration/business-unit-settings.md`, `specs/application/product/tenant-administration/data-source-connections.md`, `specs/application/product/tenant-administration/review-queues.md`, `specs/application/product/tenant-administration/audit-log-visibility.md`, `specs/application/product/tenant-administration/application-log-control.md`) for managing everything in `specs/application/technical/data-model.md § Core Data Model` that varies by business unit without needing Acme AI's involvement for routine changes. The line follows the same one drawn in `specs/application/technical/engineering-and-production-considerations.md § Engineering and Production Considerations`, mechanism stays canonical, tuning is configuration, stated here as a Decision Table over the configuration surfaces: whether a client changes each themselves, self-service, or only through Acme AI, governed, and what makes the change.

The bounds below apply to a self-service change to the surfaces they name:

- **Reference data —** a Business Unit, Geography or Competitor entry is added, disabled and has its display label renamed at any time, and its code changed only before its first use by ingested data, corrected afterward through Entity Alias. A new Geography entry specifies its parent in the hierarchy, or, for a country-level entry with no parent, its own currency instead, for the reason the currency field of `specs/application/technical/data-model.md § Core Data Model § Reference and Master Data § Geography` gives.
- **Materiality and dead-zone thresholds —** set within bounds Acme AI provides as defaults (`specs/application/product/tenant-administration/business-unit-settings.md § Business Unit Settings`), and, for the materiality thresholds, `specs/application/technical/query-service/layered-output-synthesis.md § Open Questions [Name: Default Materiality Thresholds]`.
- **The application log threshold —** lowered toward trace (`specs/application/technical/engineering-and-production-considerations.md § Engineering and Production Considerations § Security and governance`) only temporarily, expiring automatically rather than persisting.

The installation-wide audit log retention_period (`specs/application/technical/engineering-and-production-considerations.md § Engineering and Production Considerations § Security and governance`), governed, is raise-only, a decrease deliberately outside this surface entirely: the control plane service's own write path rejects any submitted value lower than the setting's current one.

The control plane service also serves read-only views of installation-wide values. The application version the installation is pinned to and each model's own pinned version (`specs/application/technical/engineering-and-production-considerations.md § Engineering and Production Considerations`), the views `specs/application/product/platform-and-compliance-operations/versioned-upgrades.md § Versioned Upgrades` promises, are read by a Control plane admin on this surface and by a Platform admin through Acme AI's own tooling. The audit log's retention_period currently in effect, the view `specs/application/product/tenant-administration/audit-log-visibility.md § Audit Log Visibility` promises, is read by a Control plane admin on this surface. The kinds of model call the installation's configuration lets reach a frontier model provider (`specs/application/technical/model-registry.md § Model registry`), the view `specs/application/product/platform-and-compliance-operations/deployment-topology.md § Deployment Topology § Frontier Models` promises, is read by a Control plane admin on this surface too. Any Control plane admin claim reads the same installation-wide value, whichever business unit it is scoped to, and no view can change what it shows. A Platform admin's read is Acme AI access through PriceHorizon's systems, recorded in the audit log like any other (`specs/application/technical/engineering-and-production-considerations.md § Engineering and Production Considerations § Security and governance`); a Control plane admin's is not.
