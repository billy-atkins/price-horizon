## Delivery Approach

Staged by what each phase is meant to prove, not by a fixed calendar.

- **Exploration —** prove the full chain end to end on the sample question: one competitor, one geography, one horizon, through to a positioning option set and its simulated outcomes. This validates the architecture before it validates the product.
- **Testing —** expand to a handful of real questions submitted by RGM and category users, with structured feedback on accuracy, speed, and trust in the output.
- **Sharpening wave —** refine the weighting rubrics and the narrative rendering model based on what testing surfaces, most of what needs adjusting will not be visible until real questions hit the system.
- **Rollout —** broaden across the full competitor, geography, and metric matrix, through PriceHorizon's own natural language interface.
