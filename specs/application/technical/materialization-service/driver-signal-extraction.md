## Vector and semantic mapping, with structured extraction

Every qualitative document ingested, regardless of source type, is, where it states an event in a driver category, chunked, embedded, and extracted into a Driver Signal (`specs/application/technical/data-model.md § Core Data Model § Vector Database Schema § Driver Signal`) stored as metadata on that same chunk in the same vector database, there is no separate structured store to keep in sync. These are the facts `specs/application/product/answer-engine/predict.md § Predict — what will the competitor do` describes as read and recorded before anyone asks. Extraction runs on every document at ingestion, on the same cadence as the corpus refresh, not filtered down to whatever seems relevant at the time, a document that looks irrelevant today may be exactly what a query needs next quarter. Extraction reads each document before chunking it: one that states no event in any driver category is not chunked or embedded, yields no Driver Signal, and is not flagged. One that does, but does not extract cleanly into that structure, an ambiguous statement, an event that fits none of its category's event types, or a signal missing what is needed to size it, an effective date, a direction and a magnitude, is flagged for review rather than forced into a guessed structure, and is not written as a Driver Signal until a reviewer resolves it. A competitor plan routed to Driver Attribution needs no magnitude, since the price-response model sizes it from the event itself. A figure a reviewer supplies or corrects cites a document that states it, ingested through its own path, or, from their own market knowledge, is written as an internal analyst note ingested through that path; either way the Driver Signal written on resolution takes that document's source_type and cites its own chunk, as `specs/application/product/tenant-administration/review-queues.md § Review Queues` promises. The chunk's own text remains in the same record purely as the citation behind the rationale trace, triangulation and driver attribution read the structured fields, they do not re-interpret the prose.
