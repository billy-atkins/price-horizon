## Business unit access, federated

Identity federates from the customer's own IdP per `specs/application/product/identity-and-access/identity-federation.md § Identity Federation`, rather than PriceHorizon picking one and asking every customer to bridge it. The customer's own IdP sits inside the customer's own environment, so federating with it needs no connection outside that environment, and an installation can run entirely within the customer's security boundary, as `specs/application/product/platform-and-compliance-operations/deployment-topology.md § Deployment Topology` promises. The IdP asserts claims or group memberships, and a governed IdP Claim Mapping (`specs/application/technical/data-model.md § Core Data Model § Reference and Master Data § IdP Claim Mapping`) translates the customer's own claim values into a business unit and one of the roles `specs/application/product/roles.md § Roles` defines, the same kind of crosswalk as `specs/application/technical/materialization-service/reconciliation.md § Product and Geography Reconciliation`, someone else's identifiers reconciled to this system's own, established once during onboarding, not resolved per query.

A Query user claim is what intent parsing checks, described in `specs/application/technical/query-service/intent-and-retrieval.md § Dynamic Retrieval and Weighting (when the question is asked)`. A Control plane admin claim is scoped to one business unit and checked by the control plane service (`specs/application/technical/engineering-and-production-considerations.md § Engineering and Production Considerations`) against whichever business unit's reference data or configuration a request tries to write, an admin for one business unit cannot write another's just because their token also grants query access to it. An Identity admin claim, the one `specs/application/technical/control-plane-service/control-plane.md § Control Plane` requires for editing the claim mapping itself, the self-service capability `specs/application/product/identity-and-access/identity-administration.md § Identity Administration` describes, is scoped to no single business unit, for the same reason `specs/application/product/roles.md § Roles` gives that role. Platform admin, the remaining role, is described in `§ Platform admin access`.

`specs/application/technical/architecture.md § Architecture Overview § Diagrams § Layer Composition` and `specs/application/technical/architecture.md § Architecture Overview § Diagrams § Trust Boundaries` render this.

## Platform admin access

`specs/application/product/roles.md § Roles` defines this role's scope, Acme AI's own staff, never the client's business configuration. It federates only through the client's own IdP, never through an identity service Acme AI runs: reaching outbound to one would work against the residency and governed-access principles this design is built on, and would be unavailable to any client whose network posture forbids it. Each customer's data stays inside their own environment by construction (`specs/application/technical/engineering-and-production-considerations.md § Engineering and Production Considerations § Security and governance`), and that same self-hosted topology puts network posture in the customer's own hands, not Acme AI's, so both of this role's access paths stay inside the customer's environment. Ordinarily the client provisions accounts for named Acme AI staff in its own IdP and asserts a platform admin claim for them, which keeps revocation entirely in the client's hands and adds no outbound dependency. Local break-glass credentials, held in the installation's own vault (`specs/application/technical/secrets-management.md § Secrets management`), are the one fallback outside that federation, for an incident where the client's own IdP cannot be reached; they need no connection outside the customer's environment either. This is the mechanism behind the client-side disable-at-any-time guarantee (`specs/application/product/platform-and-compliance-operations/deployment-topology.md`), which holds on either path. This claim is not scoped to any single business unit, the same reason `specs/application/product/roles.md § Roles` gives the role: Acme AI's own staff support and tune the whole installation, not one business unit's configuration.

`specs/application/technical/architecture.md § Architecture Overview § Diagrams § Trust Boundaries` renders this.
