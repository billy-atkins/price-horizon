## Model registry

Elasticity models, competitor price-response models, forecasting models, the driver signal extraction model used on the qualitative corpus, the narrative rendering model used to turn a rationale into prose, the driver attribution weighting rubric, the confidence scoring weighting rubric, the simulate confidence weighting rubric, and the share-response, volume, and margin models used by simulation are all exposed as versioned, callable tools with documented inputs, outputs, confidence intervals, and a last-validated date. Each versions independently of the application and of every other model in the registry (`specs/application/technical/engineering-and-production-considerations.md § Engineering and Production Considerations`), so a forecasting model can be recalibrated without touching the elasticity model or the application logic that calls both. The narrative rendering model's version is tracked for the same operational reasons as the rest, so a quality regression is a traceable, rollback-able event, but it sits outside the numeric-determinism contract, as `specs/application/technical/query-service/layered-output-synthesis.md § Layered Output Synthesis (producing the answer) § Narrative rendering` scopes it. The reasoning layer calls these tools, it does not reimplement their logic. The quantitative models, elasticity, forecasting, price-response, share-response, volume, margin, are the right fit for an MLflow-style model registry, versioned artifacts with documented inputs and outputs, rather than a custom-built abstraction. The LLM-based tools, driver signal extraction and narrative rendering, are narrower: both are schema-constrained generation, extraction into a bounded structure, rendering restricted to only the fields it is given, not open-ended reasoning, so a thin, provider-agnostic call layer like LiteLLM is the right amount of abstraction, a unified interface across model providers plus retry and fallback, without the chains, memory, and agent surface area a general LLM framework carries and this design has no use for.

`specs/application/technical/architecture.md § Architecture Overview § Diagrams § Layer Composition` and `specs/application/technical/architecture.md § Architecture Overview § Diagrams § From Evidence to Answer` render this.
