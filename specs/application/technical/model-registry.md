## Model registry

Each of these is exposed as a versioned, callable tool with documented inputs, outputs, confidence intervals, and a last-validated date:

- elasticity models, competitor price-response models, and forecasting models;
- the driver signal extraction model used on the qualitative corpus;
- the narrative rendering model used to turn a rationale into prose;
- the driver attribution weighting rubric, the confidence scoring weighting rubric, and the simulate confidence weighting rubric;
- the share-response, volume, and margin models used by simulation.

Each versions independently of the application and of every other model in the registry ([`specs/application/technical/engineering-and-production-considerations.md § Engineering and Production Considerations`](/specs/application/technical/engineering-and-production-considerations.md#engineering-and-production-considerations)), so a forecasting model can be recalibrated without touching the elasticity model or the application logic that calls both. The narrative rendering model's version is tracked for the same operational reasons as the rest, so a quality regression is a traceable, rollback-able event. The narrative rendering model sits outside the numeric-determinism contract, though, as [`specs/application/technical/query-service/layered-output-synthesis.md § Layered Output Synthesis (producing the answer) § Narrative rendering`](/specs/application/technical/query-service/layered-output-synthesis.md#narrative-rendering) scopes it. The reasoning layer calls these tools. It does not reimplement their logic.

The quantitative models (elasticity, forecasting, price-response, share-response, volume, margin) are the right fit for a model registry of versioned artifacts with documented inputs and outputs, the one [`specs/application/technical/stack.md § Technical Stack`](/specs/application/technical/stack.md#technical-stack) names, rather than a custom-built abstraction. The LLM-based tools, driver signal extraction and narrative rendering, are narrower. Both are schema-constrained generation, not open-ended reasoning: extraction into a bounded structure, and rendering restricted to only the fields it is given. So a thin, provider-agnostic call layer, in process, the one [`specs/application/technical/stack.md § Technical Stack`](/specs/application/technical/stack.md#technical-stack) names, is the right amount of abstraction. It gives a unified interface across model providers, plus retry and fallback, without the chains, memory, and agent surface area that a general LLM framework carries and this design has no use for.

A call reaches a language model in each of these kinds, each sending only its own input:

- the language model reasoning over a question ([`specs/application/technical/glossary-and-grounding.md § Guaranteed grounding`](/specs/application/technical/glossary-and-grounding.md#guaranteed-grounding)): the question and the retrieved facts and model results it cites, the forecasts and Brand A's own targets among them;
- driver signal extraction ([`specs/application/technical/materialization-service/driver-signal-extraction.md § Vector and semantic mapping, with structured extraction`](/specs/application/technical/materialization-service/driver-signal-extraction.md#vector-and-semantic-mapping-with-structured-extraction)): each qualitative document it reads, with the codes it resolves the document's entities and geographies to;
- narrative rendering ([`specs/application/technical/query-service/layered-output-synthesis.md § Layered Output Synthesis (producing the answer) § Narrative rendering`](/specs/application/technical/query-service/layered-output-synthesis.md#narrative-rendering)): the rationale record it renders;
- embedding: a document's chunks as it is ingested ([`specs/application/technical/materialization-service/driver-signal-extraction.md § Vector and semantic mapping, with structured extraction`](/specs/application/technical/materialization-service/driver-signal-extraction.md#vector-and-semantic-mapping-with-structured-extraction)), and a question's search text as evidence is retrieved ([`specs/application/technical/query-service/intent-and-retrieval.md § Dynamic Retrieval and Weighting (when the question is asked) § Parallel evidence retrieval`](/specs/application/technical/query-service/intent-and-retrieval.md#parallel-evidence-retrieval));
- semantic matching, reconciliation's fallback over product names ([`specs/application/technical/materialization-service/reconciliation.md § Product and Geography Reconciliation`](/specs/application/technical/materialization-service/reconciliation.md#product-and-geography-reconciliation)).

Every call goes through the one call layer, by default to Acme AI's own models hosted in the installation. Reasoning over a question, driver signal extraction, and narrative rendering may be served by a frontier model provider instead. The installation's configuration names each kind a frontier provider serves, with the provider's dated model version. That configuration is held in a configuration object the client's own infrastructure team owns, in the installation's Kubernetes namespace. The deployment chart reads the configuration object, and no upgrade writes it, whether the upgrade is Acme AI-managed or self-managed. Only those kinds' calls leave the installation, each carrying only its own input.

The version serving each call is recorded, Acme AI's own hosted model's and a frontier provider's alike, so an answer shows which models served it. Reasoning's and narrative rendering's versions are recorded in the provenance of the answer they serve ([`specs/application/technical/data-model.md § Core Data Model § Pipeline and Answer Artifacts § Answer Provenance`](/specs/application/technical/data-model.md#answer-provenance)). Driver signal extraction's version is recorded on each signal it produces ([`specs/application/technical/data-model.md § Core Data Model § Vector Database Schema § Driver Signal`](/specs/application/technical/data-model.md#driver-signal)). Embedding and semantic matching never leave the installation, because a corpus is searched only with the model that embedded it, and matching runs over every catalog the installation holds. No quantitative model or weighting rubric is ever served outside the installation.

A Control plane admin sees which kinds are named ([`specs/application/technical/control-plane-service/control-plane.md § Control Plane`](/specs/application/technical/control-plane-service/control-plane.md#control-plane)). As the installation starts, before any call reaches a frontier model provider, the control plane service records a change from the set it last recorded. It records the change as one frontier_models_changed entry for the installation, with the kinds and versions named before and after the change ([`specs/application/technical/data-model.md § Core Data Model § Audit Log § Audit Log Entry`](/specs/application/technical/data-model.md#audit-log-entry)). While the control plane service cannot record that entry, no call reaches a frontier model provider. This carries out [`specs/application/product/platform-and-compliance-operations/deployment-topology.md § Deployment Topology § Frontier Models`](/specs/application/product/platform-and-compliance-operations/deployment-topology.md#frontier-models).

[`specs/application/technical/architecture.md § Architecture Overview § Diagrams § Layer Composition`](/specs/application/technical/architecture.md#layer-composition), [`specs/application/technical/architecture.md § Architecture Overview § Diagrams § From Evidence to Answer`](/specs/application/technical/architecture.md#from-evidence-to-answer) and [`specs/application/technical/architecture.md § Architecture Overview § Diagrams § Trust Boundaries`](/specs/application/technical/architecture.md#trust-boundaries) render this.

## Open Questions

**Records:**

- **Name:** Model Freshness in Predict
  **Open Question:** Does Predict's confidence reflect when the forecasting models behind it were last validated, as Simulate's does for the models behind it?
  **Provisional Answer:** No. Only Simulate's confidence reads a model's last-validated date, by the model freshness component of [`specs/application/technical/query-service/layered-output-synthesis.md § Layered Output Synthesis (producing the answer) § Confidence and rationale package, extended`](/specs/application/technical/query-service/layered-output-synthesis.md#confidence-and-rationale-package-extended). Predict's confidence reads no model's last-validated date. A forecasting model's performance is watched only by the drift monitoring [`specs/application/technical/evaluation-and-monitoring.md § Evaluation and monitoring`](/specs/application/technical/evaluation-and-monitoring.md#evaluation-and-monitoring) describes, the trend in forecast error, outside any confidence score.
  **Impacts:** Predict's confidence ([`specs/application/technical/predict-computation.md § Predict Computation § Confidence and rationale package`](/specs/application/technical/predict-computation.md#confidence-and-rationale-package)); and every registered model's last-validated date ([`§ Model registry`](#model-registry)).
