## Risks and Mitigations

Each risk in this section is the failure mode of a Constraint already established where its mechanism lives. Gathered here as one Constraint table, so a reviewer can scan every safeguard in the design at once and confirm each one actually traces to something that enforces it, not just a sentence asserting it.

| Constraint | Applies to | Enforced by |
|---|---|---|
| An unrecognized conflict between qualitative and quantitative evidence, big enough to count as a price move, is never resolved silently | Triangulation and driver attribution | `specs/application/technical/predict-computation.md § Predict Computation § Triangulation and conflict checks` selects the signals, `specs/application/technical/predict-computation.md § Predict Computation § Driver Attribution` sizes and flags the conflict, `specs/application/technical/predict-computation.md § Predict Computation § Confidence and rationale packaging` reduces and caps the score |
| Licensed data is never combined or displayed beyond its licensing terms | Syndicated data | `specs/application/technical/engineering-and-production-considerations.md § Engineering and Production Considerations § Security and governance`, access control layer |
| A model's confidence reflects when it was last validated, never assumed indefinitely | Every registered model | `specs/application/technical/evaluation-and-monitoring.md § Evaluation and monitoring`, golden-question harness, model version dates |
| Every claim in rendered narrative text traces to a field in the rationale record | Narrative rendering | `specs/application/technical/query-service/layered-output-synthesis.md § Layered Output Synthesis (producing the answer) § Narrative rendering`, CI harness |
| Position never exposes a single "best option" | The Position stage | `specs/application/technical/query-service/layered-output-synthesis.md § Layered Output Synthesis (producing the answer) § Option Consolidation` Constraint (floor of two), the API and UI contract |
| An allocated figure is never displayed with the confidence of a directly sourced forecast | Drill-down below the materialized grid | `specs/application/technical/query-service/layered-output-synthesis.md § Layered Output Synthesis (producing the answer) § Drill-down mechanics` |
| A low-confidence or incorrect product match never silently produces a wrong price gap or comparison | Product and Geography Reconciliation | `specs/application/technical/materialization-service/reconciliation.md § Product and Geography Reconciliation` Algorithm, step 4 |
| Every event type has exactly one declared `routes_to` consumer | Event Type Taxonomy | `specs/application/technical/predict-computation.md § Predict Computation § Signal routing` Decision Table, golden-question harness |
| The decomposition graph is never reused, fabricated, or hidden below the materialized grid's geography | Decomposition graph | `specs/application/technical/query-service/layered-output-synthesis.md § Layered Output Synthesis (producing the answer) § Drill-down mechanics` |
| A self-service change never breaks continuity or invalidates in-flight materialized data | Entity reference data, Entity Alias | `specs/application/technical/data-model.md § Core Data Model § Reference and Master Data § Entity Alias` |
| Break-glass credentials are never stored outside a secrets vault or used for routine access | Platform admin access | `specs/application/technical/secrets-management.md § Secrets management` |
| One business unit's competitively sensitive documents are never reachable through another's query, even from a bug in application filtering code | Vector database retrieval | `specs/application/technical/data-model.md § Core Data Model` namespace partitioning, `specs/application/technical/secrets-management.md § Secrets management` per-namespace credentials |
| Two materialization runs for the same business unit never race against each other and overwrite a fresher result with a staler one | Forecast materialization | `specs/application/technical/materialization-service/forecast-materialization.md § Forecast materialization` per-business-unit concurrency control |
| A confidence score is never a black box, it traces to a declared, versioned formula and a stated conflict-override rule, not an opaque or trained one | The guardrail guarantee | `specs/application/technical/predict-computation.md § Predict Computation § Confidence and rationale packaging` Algorithm and Constraint |
| A Simulated Outcome's confidence is never displayed as more certain than the Predict-stage forecast it is built on | Simulated Outcome | `specs/application/technical/query-service/layered-output-synthesis.md § Layered Output Synthesis (producing the answer) § Confidence and rationale packaging, extended` Constraint (step 9 cap) |
| A forecast is checked against what actually happened once it's knowable, not asserted as accurate and left unexamined | Model accuracy over time | `specs/application/technical/materialization-service/forecast-materialization.md § Realized outcome tracking` Algorithm |
