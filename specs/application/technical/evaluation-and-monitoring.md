## Evaluation and monitoring

A golden question is a curated input scenario paired with an assertion about a structural property the answer must have. It makes no claim about what a real-world forecast will turn out to be. When a golden question is written, no ground truth exists for the forecast. Ground truth exists only for what the system's own declared rules promise the system will do with that input. A Decision Table's rows, an Algorithm's steps, a State Machine's states, and a Constraint's invariant can each be checked. A golden question is that check, exercised end to end through the running system rather than read off the page. A new row in a Decision Table, a new step in an Algorithm, or a new Constraint gets at least one golden question engineered to exercise it. Whoever adds the row, step, or Constraint adds the golden question, the same discipline as writing a test alongside new code. No separate curatorial role adds it.

For example, a question engineered to produce exactly one flagged conflict asserts that the confidence scoring Constraint's ceiling caps the score ([`specs/application/technical/predict-computation.md § Predict Computation § Confidence and rationale package § A flagged conflict's ceiling`](/specs/application/technical/predict-computation.md#a-flagged-conflicts-ceiling)). It does not assert that the resulting number matches any claimed real-world truth. The suite runs in CI whenever a model, prompt template, or data pipeline version changes. Every assertion has to hold before that version ships.

This suite does not validate forecast accuracy against what happened. That validation needs real, observed outcomes collected over time, and [`specs/application/technical/materialization-service/forecast-materialization.md § Realized outcome check`](/specs/application/technical/materialization-service/forecast-materialization.md#realized-outcome-check) is the mechanism for it. Nor is this suite the same thing as the product spec's Gherkin scenarios ([`specs/application/product/answer-engine/predict.md`](/specs/application/product/answer-engine/predict.md), [`specs/application/product/answer-engine/position.md`](/specs/application/product/answer-engine/position.md), [`specs/application/product/answer-engine/simulate.md`](/specs/application/product/answer-engine/simulate.md), [`specs/application/product/answer-engine/drill-down.md`](/specs/application/product/answer-engine/drill-down.md), and every other product capability, each with its own `Test Scenarios` section). Those scenarios are black-box checks that a capability delivers what it promises to a user, whichever internal mechanism produced it. This suite is white-box. It checks that one specific mechanism, such as a Decision Table row, an Algorithm step, or a Constraint, fires correctly. Both kinds of check matter, and neither substitutes for the other.

Beyond the golden-question regression suite, the human review queue for upstream data quality ([`specs/application/technical/data-model.md § Core Data Model § Pipeline and Answer Artifacts § Review Queue Item`](/specs/application/technical/data-model.md#review-queue-item)) and drift monitoring on the underlying models keep the system honest as data and markets shift. Drift monitoring watches the trend in forecast_error across accumulated Realized Outcomes ([`specs/application/technical/materialization-service/forecast-materialization.md § Open Questions [Name: Recalibration]`](/specs/application/technical/materialization-service/forecast-materialization.md#open-questions)), so it is more than a named intention. An elasticity model trained on pre-inflation data is not the same model six quarters later.

### Forecast golden questions

For the scheduled-grid golden case, configure two tracked competitors with two tracked brands each, two geographies and every standard horizon (1, 3, 6 and 12 months) for one business unit. Each of the 32 distinct keys must have a stored forecast at the run's pinned versions. Ask for one of those keys and inspect the Question run. It must reach positioning with the stored forecast and rationale, with zero live Predict model calls. Ask again with only the resolved brand changed. Each lookup must select its own brand’s bundle, even when the two forecast values coincide.

Ask for an outside-grid horizon with no stored forecast, then repeat the question at the same pinned versions. The first run must persist the live forecast before positioning. The second must return that forecast and rationale with zero live Predict model calls. Compare the stored key, the forecast and the rationale on both runs. A matching displayed number alone cannot demonstrate reuse.

For a baseline with list price 100 (interval 97 to 104), promotion depth 10 (interval 8 to 15), rebate 2 and surcharge 1, expect effective price 89, bounded by 81 and 95. The downside and upside offsets are 8 and 6. Use a known future rebate of 2 in place of a last observed rebate of 3, with no future surcharge term and a last observed surcharge of 1. A term learned after the pinned vintage cannot change this run. The rationale must identify the rebate term and the dated surcharge observation. Feed the same observed history into two different requested horizons with different pinned model estimates. Each horizon must receive its own estimate on both scheduled and live calls.

After a stable-price threshold edit, ask for an existing scheduled cell before and after its next successful refresh. The first lookup must keep the old forecast and computation version; the second must select the refreshed one. An outside-grid query before the refresh uses the new threshold and cannot reuse a live bundle with the old configuration.

For two competitors in the same market and window, give only the second competitor an announced price change and give the business unit an applicable tax change. Each forecast uses the tax signal, and only the second baseline uses the price announcement. Check both paths. For a tax increase of 1 to 2 percentage points on a documented base of 20 per unit, the midpoint is 1.5 points and its direct effect is 0.30 per unit before weighting. A charge of 1.5 currency units per unit has an effect of 1.50. The evidence preserves the source units and the calculation.

Give competitor plans an otherwise complete trigger with no reported magnitude. Its pinned response model must run. A stated zero must remain distinguishable from absence in the model input. With no trigger, the model must receive no call. For a direct driver with two equal and opposite nonzero effects, retain both source references even though the displayed net correction is zero.

Give two invoked simulation models equal lowest interval-width scores and one of them the unique lowest freshness score. Every candidate must share a rationale naming both limiting versions for confidence and only the latter for freshness. The trace must still distinguish the calls and inputs for each candidate.

Exercise the no-qualitative-evidence case on both scheduled and live computation. Use a snapshot freshness score strictly between the rubric's endpoints and an ordinary freshness weight below one. In both runs the score must equal snapshot freshness, and the stored explanation must mark credibility and agreement inapplicable.

Stage a refresh with a new vintage and new model versions while the previous grid is still published. A first-time outside-grid request must run with the previous publication's vintage and models and the current applicable settings. A failed refresh preserves those pins. After successful publication, repeat the request and require a new stored forecast carrying the new publication's vintage and models. Verify the earlier forecast and its rationale remain traceable to their original inputs.

For sibling brands A and B under one competitor, give A a deeper promotion and B a list-price increase for the same geography and window. Inspect the inputs on both scheduled and live paths. Only A’s promotional cadence model receives the promotional calendar change. Only B’s list-price model receives the announced price change. Neither enters driver attribution. Add an explicitly portfolio-wide promotion and require both brands’ promotional models to receive it. An announcement with unresolved brand scope stays in review.

At the same pinned snapshot, compare a forecast without qualitative evidence to one with a single agreeing licensed source. Use ordinary credibility, freshness and agreement weights of 0.5, 0.3 and 0.2 and component scores of 0.6, 0.8 and 1 on a unit scale. The first forecast must score 0.8 on snapshot freshness alone. The second must score 0.74 and retain all three scores, weights and the source citation. Check the scheduled and live paths.

Publish a grid with a competitor-response model pinned. Ask an outside-grid question with no competitor-plan trigger. The model is present in forecast_model_versions and absent from the call trace. Repeat at the same pins and require the stored row, rationale and zero Predict calls. Change the published response-model version alone and repeat. The key must change even if the new computation again has no trigger.
