## Vision

Picture a Revenue Growth Management (RGM) leader typing one sentence into a box: "In 6 months, where will Competitor Brand's price be in Texas, and what will happen?" Ten seconds later they have a defensible answer, not a dashboard they have to interpret themselves. A confidence-scored forecast, a plain-language explanation of what is driving it, a map of how the number breaks down by market, a chart of the underlying forces, a small set of positioning options for Brand A instead of one imposed answer, and the simulated share, volume, and margin trade-off between them, with the ability to click into any of it and keep going, down to a county, or across to a different metric entirely. And none of it is a magic number: the forecast shows how it was built, from the competitor's own price trend plus each driver's push on it, and its confidence score opens to why it deserves to be believed, or why not yet.

That is the destination: RGM questions answered with the rigor of an analyst, the speed of a search box, and the traceability to survive a finance or retailer conversation.
