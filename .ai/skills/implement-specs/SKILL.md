# Implementing the specs

The specs say what the code does. This skill writes the code that does it. It starts where `design-specs` ends, from applied specs. It designs the code and the tests that bring the specs to life, as `design-specs` designs a change to the specs. Once this skill's best effort is exhausted, `verify-spec-implementation`'s full run audits the code, as `audit-specs`' run audits the specs. The steps guard against a unit doing more or less than the spec it cites, a citation higher or lower than what the unit carries out, governed code changed with no spec behind the change, and wiring that carries out a rule no spec states.

Using this skill, you are the builder (`specs/methodology/glossary.md`). The opening of `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine` names the sections that set your role against your reviewers, the order of your work and when you stop for the user. Read it again each time you resume the work, as that opening has it.

## Workflow

Each step applying a rule carries its point-of-use citation (`specs/methodology/sourcing-and-citation.md § One Home Per Fact`). The script is `scripts/implement-specs.py`, the only one this skill runs. Its `--help` gives its actions. Its workstack actions read this skill's plans folder and the other plans folders beside it, and its validation review draws only on the design and the canon's declarations of its form. A name in another folder is printed as that folder's name, a slash and the design's Name.

Each step runs in the phase its work belongs to. A step deciding what the code does runs on the design's plan steps, before validation. A step writing the code, or checking what was written, runs at the apply (`specs/methodology/design-workflow.md § The Order of a Design's Work § The Apply`).

### Start from the entry point

Read `specs/AGENTS.md` and the glossary before any step. Descend only as far as a step needs, per `specs/methodology/scope.md § Progressive Disclosure`. Each time you resume the work, read the opening of `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine` again, as that opening has it. Propose a new branch for the work, and create it only on the user's approval, per `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § A New Branch`.

### Start from the specs

Design the code from the specs as they stand, or as a design named in the design's Depends On leaves them, the frame's Scope stating what this design relies on from it, per `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document`. Do so the first time, or after the specs change. Write code only from specs that stand applied. Move the design's Status to in-progress as you take it up. Append each entry of the design's log through `scripts/implement-specs.py --append-entry`, here and at each check and event that follows, as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § Design Log Entry` has each appended. A change to what governed code must do that no spec yet states goes to the specs first, per `specs/methodology/code.md § Changing Code`. Code written ahead of its spec is code no spec governs.

### Write the design

Write the design as the persona `specs/methodology/spec-placement.md § Personas` gives the code. Copy `references/design-document-form-template.md` to a file of its own under `.ai/plans/implement-specs/`, per `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document § A Design Changing Code`. Scope the design to one product module, per `specs/methodology/spec-placement.md § A Product Module`. Fill it from that module's specs, reading what `specs/methodology/spec-placement.md § A Product Module` sets, fresh, as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Reading Fresh` has it, in the order `specs/methodology/scope.md § Progressive Disclosure` sets. Follow a section's citations rather than loading whole files beyond the module. Stop at a gap `specs/methodology/spec-placement.md § A Product Module` names, or at a local environment the specs do not give, per `specs/methodology/spec-placement.md § Environments`. Present what stopped you to the user as a gap the specs must put right, as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Decides` has a call of the user's asked.

Agree on the design goals with the user, then present the design frame, as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § The Goals and the Frame` has both. Where the design's Depends On names a design, the frame's Scope states what this design relies on from each, per `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document`. Record the goals as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § Design Goal` has them. Only once the user agrees on the goals and agrees to or adjusts the frame, state the design's steps, each a record of the type `specs/methodology/working-files.md § Design Plan Step` declares, its Objectives the state the code holds once it is applied, never the code that brings it about. Before stating each step's outcome, zoom out and find the rule's reach, as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Measure Twice, Cut Once` has it, and name it in the step's What and in the Target. Find the code that carries a spec out by searching for its annotations rather than reading the codebase. The steps reach:
- each unit of governed code and its app-spec annotations, the narrowest citations that together state what the unit carries out, per `specs/methodology/code.md § Citing the Specs From Code`;
- the wiring that connects the units, per `specs/methodology/code.md § Governed Code and Wiring Code`;
- the tests that check the specs, per `specs/methodology/code.md § Tests`.

### Stop at annotations out of sync

Whenever you meet existing annotations, while writing the design or the code, read them against `specs/methodology/code.md § Changing Code`. That section says when they stop the work and how the work resumes. Present each annotation that stops the work to the user with its file, its line and what it fails: the rule in `specs/methodology/code.md` or the spec it no longer matches. Ask it as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Decides` has a call of the user's asked. Name the fix-it design the user decides in this design's Depends On, and state in the frame's Scope what this design relies on from it, per `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document`, so the workstack holds the wait. Resume as revising what waited has it, against the code and the specs as they then stand.

### Sort each steer, and each gap you find

The specs are the starting point, so you draft the design of the code and the tests from them on your own. The user approves the design or steers it, as the human in the loop for the code as for the specs. Record each steer, per `specs/methodology/working-files.md § Steering Decision`, and sort it first. Confirm a steer changing the design's goals or frame with the user before applying it, and have the user approve the change before the work goes on, as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § The Goals and the Frame` has it. Record any other steer as given. A steer deciding how the code carries out the specs belongs to this design. A steer changing what a spec states, whether a promise, a rule or a mechanism, is a decision about the specs. Spawn it as a design changing the specs, with the steer as that design's Source where the steer asks for the work, per `specs/methodology/working-files.md § Steering Decision`. Name the spawned design in this design's Depends On, so this design is blocked until the spawned one finishes, and state in the frame's Scope what this design relies on from it, per `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document`. A gap you find yourself, a spec the code needs that none states, goes to the user to spawn the same way. Any other change of its own the work turns up, work no goal reaches, goes to the user to spawn or log, a change to working files alone logged, per `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document`. A change of its own is instead taken into this design only where the user first widens the goals to take it in, as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § The Goals and the Frame` has it. Ask the user about a gap, about a change of its own, and about a steer that could either decide how the code carries out the specs or change what a spec states. Ask each as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Decides` has a call asked. For a change of its own, offer the widening of the goals beside spawning and logging. Record what this design leaves unaddressed as the Out of Scope row of `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document` has it. A follow-up logged for it names this design (`specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Follow-up`).

### Revise what waited, once the specs it waited on change

When `scripts/implement-specs.py --show-workstack` lists this design to revise, move it back to in-progress if it is approved. Its work then goes on as `specs/methodology/design-workflow.md § The Order of a Design's Work § The Work of a Design` routes it. Move back each approved design that `--check-workstack` then reports as depending on this one. Take in what each finished design named in this design's Depends On settled, or why it was dropped, against the specs as they now stand. Then remove that design from Depends On, and from the frame's Scope what this design relied on from it, per `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document`. The code is designed against the specs it will carry out, not the specs it was first drafted from.

### Design, Refactor, Refine, then the reviews

Take the steps through the Design, Refactor, Refine process, per `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Design`, `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Refactor` and `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Refine`, and again for each step a change moves, as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Measure Twice, Cut Once` has it. Get them working, refactor them until they stop moving, and refine them by reading each cited spec against the unit a step states: no unit carries out more than it cites, and no wiring carries out a rule a spec states (`specs/methodology/code.md § Governed Code and Wiring Code`). Hold to the goals the work you add as the design goes on, as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § The Goals and the Frame` has it.

Then, at the end of `specs/methodology/design-workflow.md § The Order of a Design's Work § The Work of a Design [States.State: drafting]` and of changing, before the design goes to validating, make your self-review for the adversarial review that follows, as your row of `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Checks What` sets. Run `--check-cited-headings` for each spec the design cites, and `--check-target-kind` for its Target, as part of it, and, at drafting, `--show-reach` for the wording its Retired Phrases (`specs/methodology/working-files.md § Retired Phrase`) record. A heading added by a design that this design's Depends On names is reported until that design is applied, and is the expected report where the frame's Scope states this design relies on it. So the work runs Design, Refactor, Refine, your self-review, the validation, then the adversarial review.

At drafting too, before its first adversarial review, offer the user the reviewer's model and reasoning effort, once for a design, as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Each Review's Model and Effort` has the user choose them. Where the user's approval ends the review before any round, make that offer before its post-apply review. The effort defaults to the one `specs/methodology/design-workflow.md § A Design's Reviews` gives code, as `specs/methodology/design-workflow.md § A Review's Effort` has it.

At `specs/methodology/design-workflow.md § The Order of a Design's Work § The Work of a Design [States.State: validating]`, run `scripts/implement-specs.py --check-workstack` first, and put right what it reports. Then run the validation review: `--check-design` before an adversarial round, and `--stamp-validation` on the way to the user's approval, which stamps the design once it passes. Fix each problem the script reports, and run it again.

At the design's reviewing state, start a cold agent with the brief `specs/methodology/design-workflow.md § A Review's Brief` holds, naming the adversarial review, its blanks filled and nothing added. It runs on the model and at the effort the user chose, as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Each Review's Model and Effort` has the choice kept. Its report is a file under `.ai/tmp/implement-specs/`, as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § The Working Files` gives a skill's output. The review's task is `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § The Adversarial Review` and its row of `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Checks What`. Settle each round of the adversarial review per `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Settling a Round`: `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Settling a Round § The Test` first, then `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Settling a Round § The Round at Its Causes` and `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Settling a Round § After the Last Round`. Mark each finding the test keeps, as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Converging, or Not` has it, and count each run, as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Converging, or Not § The Runs` has it. Before each fix, zoom out as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Measure Twice, Cut Once` has it, and write the fix whole across every place stating the fact it changes. At changing, prove the change by your self-review, never by rereading the fix alone, as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Settling a Round § The Round at Its Causes § Change and Prove` has it. Add your settlement to the review's report, as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § Design Log Entry` names it, and bring each review observation to the user with the round, as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Decides` has it.

Where the user approves the plan anywhere in its work, with apply or without, let the state in hand finish its own work, as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § The Adversarial Review § The Plan's Approval` has it. A round still running or being settled is left with its findings as they are. Then go on to validating, as the same state machine routes it. Once stamped, the design is approved. It is applied where the approval said so, once `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document § The States It Moves Through` lets it move into applying. Once the approval has ended the review, the user may ask for another round at awaiting approval.

### Ask for approval

Show the user the plan with its summary, as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § What the User Is Shown` has it, and ask for approval as a choice of approved, approved and apply, or not approved. An alteration the user asks for takes the route `specs/methodology/design-workflow.md § The Order of a Design's Work § The Work of a Design` gives it. Where the user's approval had ended the review, also offer another round. Each choice moves the design as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document § The States It Moves Through` has a design move. Offer approved and apply only once that section would let the design move into applying.

### Write the code

Once `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document § The States It Moves Through` lets the design's Status move to applying, move it. At `specs/methodology/design-workflow.md § The Order of a Design's Work § The Apply [States.State: writing]`, before writing what the steps state, zoom out as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Measure Twice, Cut Once` has it. Write the reach you find whole, within the Target and with no change asking its own approval, since the user's approval of the plan authorizes it (`specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document`). Read each file fresh before writing code into it, a direct edit no exception, as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Reading Fresh` has a file read. Take what you write through Design, Refactor, Refine, as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Measure Twice, Cut Once` has it. Write from the files as they stand, the changes a send-back kept among them, as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document § The States It Moves Through § A Send-back` states them, keeping or removing each uncommitted change as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document § Uncommitted Changes` has it at the writing. Take in an edit the user makes as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § Steering Decision § Telling a Steer Apart` has it, checking it as your row of `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Checks What` sets. Write:
- the governed code, with its annotations above each unit, per `specs/methodology/code.md § Citing the Specs From Code`;
- the wiring, carrying no annotations;
- the tests.

Write them in American English, per `specs/methodology/code.md § Changing Code`. As you write, run the targeted form of the local environment's test tasks on the tests citing the specs the code carries out, per `specs/methodology/spec-placement.md § Environments`. Change governed code only through this design, per `specs/methodology/code.md § Changing Code`, and only as the design's changes are, per `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document`. A change the steps do not hold is a finding that the plan cannot be delivered as its steps state it. Take it to the user, as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Decides` has a call asked. The user's triage of it as lying in the plan sends the design back with the code written kept in place, as `specs/methodology/design-workflow.md § The Order of a Design's Work § The Apply [States.State: sent back]` has it, and nothing is reverted. A change no spec states goes back to the specs. At every state of the apply, before acting on anything the user says, tell it apart by `specs/methodology/working-files.md § Steering Decision § Telling a Steer Apart`, as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Measure Twice, Cut Once` has it, and act by its route: feedback on the applied result is taken in while writing, never as a steer. Where the user's words ask for a route the canon does not have, say so and name the canon's route before acting.

### Check what you wrote, then the post-apply review, the audit and the commit

Before anyone else reads the code, make your self-review for the post-apply review that follows, as your row of `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Checks What` sets. As part of it, check that:
- every unit is written;
- every annotation names a heading that exists, is sorted, and is as narrow as the unit (`specs/methodology/code.md § Citing the Specs From Code`);
- the wiring carries no spec's rule (`specs/methodology/code.md § Governed Code and Wiring Code`);
- what you wrote is in American English (`specs/methodology/code.md § Changing Code`);
- every build, lint and test task of the local environment passes, after the targeted tests citing the specs the code carries out, which you ran as you worked, per `specs/methodology/spec-placement.md § Environments`;
- `--check-retired-wording` finds none of the retired wording left in the Target, and `--check-changed-files` lists no file changed outside it that is none of the design's changes.

`specs/methodology/spec-placement.md § Environments` also says what to present where the specs give no such tasks. Record the check in your self-review report (`specs/methodology/working-files.md § Design Log Entry`).

Mark each finding the test keeps, as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Converging, or Not` has it. Count the run of post-apply rounds, as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Converging, or Not § The Runs` has it. Before each fix, zoom out as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Measure Twice, Cut Once` has it, and write the fix whole across every place stating the fact it changes. Read each file fresh before fixing it, as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Reading Fresh` has a file read. Before the next round, or before the user's review where none follows, prove the change by your self-review, never by rereading the fix alone, as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Settling a Round § The Round at Its Causes § Change and Prove` has it. Bring each review observation, and each finding lying in the plan, to the user, as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Decides` has it.
