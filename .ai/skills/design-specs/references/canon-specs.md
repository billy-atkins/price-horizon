# Designing the canon

The steps a change to the canon owes beyond the Workflow of `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md`, read from its fork, in the order that Workflow reaches them. Each names the step it joins. Every other specification is written and checked against the canon's rules, so a change to them reaches further than it looks.

## Workflow

**Find everything applying a rule before changing it —** within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Find the fact's existing home`: the steps of the skills citing its section, the audits table and the table of operating rules naming it, the rules building on it, the audit script's rule labels, and the application specs written under it, each where a change to the rule lands. A retitled section takes every citation of it along, per `specs/methodology/sourcing-and-citation.md § Titling a Heading`, and a moved one changes the path in each.

**Coin a term only once it passes the test —** within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Write the text in the form the rules give it`: `specs/methodology/glossary.md § Writing an Entry`, and in the form it gives.

**Add or change a skill —** within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Write the text in the form the rules give it`: in the form `specs/methodology/skills.md § Authoring a Skill` gives, registered by the test `specs/methodology/skills.md § Registered Skills` states. A skill's instructions, and both `AGENTS.md` files, stay as `specs/methodology/scope.md § Agent Agnostic` asks.

**Give an added or changed rule its check —** after `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Write the text in the form the rules give it`: `specs/methodology/scope.md § Rules and Skills`, in `.ai/skills/audit-specs/`' audits table or its table of operating rules carried out by a step.

**Give an added rule its point-of-use citation —** after `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Write the text in the form the rules give it`, at the step of this skill's Workflow or references where the rule is applied, per `specs/methodology/sourcing-and-citation.md § One Home Per Fact`.

**Keep the method's own files in step —** within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Synchronize whatever restates what you changed`: the methodology's `architecture.md`, per `specs/methodology/sourcing-and-citation.md § Keeping Renderings in Step`, and `index.md`, per `specs/methodology/spec-placement.md § Where a File Goes`, the tree `specs/methodology/scope.md § The Shape of the Scope` draws, and the playbook and index in `specs/AGENTS.md § Writing specs`.

## What a canon change leaves behind

The application specs were written against the rule as it stood. A rule tightened, renamed or moved can leave them in breach, and nothing in the canon change itself shows it: every canon file is correct, and the breach sits in files the change never touched. The audit script finds such a breach only where it checks the rule, a field's key or an Open Questions section's form among them; the rest is found only by a reading audit of the application specs against the sections the change altered, which the audit after this change is applied runs beside its canon audits, as `.ai/skills/audit-specs/SKILL.md`'s fork has it. Before applying, sweep the application specs by meaning for what the changed rule governs, as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine (DRR)` has a scope swept; whatever now breaches it is the application entry `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Entry` has depend on this one.
