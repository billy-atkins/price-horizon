# Designing the canon

The steps a change to the canon owes beyond the Workflow of `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md`, read from its fork, in the order that Workflow reaches them. Each names the step it joins. Every other specification is written and checked against the canon's rules, so a change to them reaches further than it looks.

## Workflow

**Find everything applying a rule before changing it —** within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Find the fact's existing home`: the steps of the skills citing its section, the audits table and the table of operating rules naming it, the rules building on it and the audit script's rule labels, each where a change to the rule lands; the application specs written under it are reviewed by this Workflow's step reviewing them. A retitled section takes every citation of it along, per `specs/methodology/sourcing-and-citation.md § Titling a Heading`, and a moved one changes the path in each.

**Coin a term only once it passes the test —** within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Write the text in the form the rules give it`: `specs/methodology/glossary.md § Writing an Entry`, and in the form it gives, any abbreviation it is given used only as `specs/methodology/glossary.md § Using an Abbreviation` allows.

**Draw no example from the application specs —** within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Write the text in the form the rules give it`: per `specs/methodology/spec-style.md § Clear Prose`, its rule on an example in the canon.

**Add or change a skill —** within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Write the text in the form the rules give it`: in the form `specs/methodology/skills.md § Authoring a Skill` gives, registered by the test `specs/methodology/skills.md § Registered Skills` states. A skill's instructions, and both `AGENTS.md` files, stay as `specs/methodology/scope.md § Agent Agnostic` asks.

**Give an added or changed rule its check —** after `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Write the text in the form the rules give it`: `specs/methodology/scope.md § Rules and Skills`, in `.ai/skills/audit-specs/`' audits table or its table of operating rules carried out by a step, an operating rule's check being complete only once the step carrying it out cites it.

**Give an added rule its point-of-use citation —** after `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Write the text in the form the rules give it`, at the step of this skill's Workflow or references where the rule is applied, per `specs/methodology/sourcing-and-citation.md § One Home Per Fact`.

**Keep the method's own files in step —** within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Synchronize whatever restates what you changed`: the methodology's `architecture.md`, per `specs/methodology/sourcing-and-citation.md § Keeping Renderings in Step`, and `index.md`, per `specs/methodology/spec-placement.md § Where a File Goes`, the tree `specs/methodology/scope.md § The Shape of the Scope` draws, and the playbook and index in `specs/AGENTS.md § Writing specs`.

**Review the application specs the change reaches —** after `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Synchronize whatever restates what you changed`, so each revision reruns it, for the reason `§ What a canon change leaves behind` gives: read each application file the sections the change alters could govern, for what those sections now require, by meaning as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine` has a scope swept, and record what the review finds, and how it was done, in the design's Impact on the Application Specs, per `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document`, editing none of those files. Hand the application work it records on as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document` has a change needing both kinds split: to the application design this one was spawned from, or to the user, per `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Hand a change of its own to the user to spawn or log`.

**Settle what the post-apply audit finds in the application specs as application work —** within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Refactor and refine, then the adversarial review, then apply and audit`: add each such finding to the application work the review handed on, or hand it on the same way, per `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document`, editing none of those files.

## What a canon change leaves behind

The application specs were written against the rule as it stood. A rule tightened, renamed or moved can leave them in breach, and nothing in the canon change itself shows it: every canon file is correct, and the breach sits in files the change never touched. The audit script finds such a breach only where it checks the rule, a field's key or an Open Questions section's form among them, and the post-apply audit's reading of the application specs, as `.ai/skills/audit-specs/SKILL.md`'s fork has it, only once the change has landed. A review before the change is approved is what lets the application work be known, and handed on, while the canon design is still shaped.
