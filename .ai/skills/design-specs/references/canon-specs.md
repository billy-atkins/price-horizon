# Designing the canon

These are the steps a change to the canon owes beyond the Workflow of `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md`. They are read from that Workflow's fork, in the order the Workflow reaches the last step each joins. Each names the step it joins, or, where it decides and writes, the deciding step and the writing step. Every other specification is written and checked against the canon's rules, so a change to those rules reaches further than it looks.

## Workflow

**Find everything applying a rule before changing it.** Within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Find the fact's existing home`, find each place a change to the rule lands: the steps of the skills citing its section, the audits table and the table of operating rules naming it, the rules building on it, and the audit script's labels naming the section. Zoom out to find them, as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Measure Twice, Cut Once` has the builder zoom out to a rule's reach. The application specs written under the rule are reviewed by this Workflow's step reviewing them. A retitled section takes every citation of it along, per `specs/methodology/sourcing-and-citation.md § Titling a Heading`, and a moved one changes the path in each.

**Take a standard's form before the canon's own.** Within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Check whether a construct applies before writing prose`, choose the form a need takes as `specs/methodology/sourcing-and-citation.md § External Reference § Which Form a Need Takes` has it, and give each departure from a standard its row as `specs/methodology/sourcing-and-citation.md § External Reference § A Mapping onto a Standard` has it. Where the change moves a registered standard's version or registers one, judge again each row whose Departs For holds the standard's gap, as that section has it, and record the judgment in the design.

**Give an added or changed rule its check.** Within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § State each step as its outcome`, give each rule a plan step adds or changes its check, as `specs/methodology/scope.md § Rules and Skills` has it, in that plan step's Objectives. The check goes in `.ai/skills/audit-specs/`' audits table, or in its table of operating rules carried out by a step. An operating rule's check is complete only once the step carrying it out cites it.

**Give an added rule its point-of-use citation.** Within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § State each step as its outcome`, name the step of this skill's Workflow or references where each rule a plan step adds is applied, and state its citation there in the plan step's Objectives, per `specs/methodology/sourcing-and-citation.md § One Home Per Fact`.

**Review the application specs the change reaches.** Run this review after `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § State each step as its outcome`, so each revision of the steps reruns it, for the reason `§ What a canon change leaves behind` gives. Read each application file that the sections the steps alter could govern, for what those sections will require once the steps' Objectives hold. Sweep by meaning, as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § The Goals and the Frame` has a scope swept. Record what the review finds, and how it was done, in the design's Impact on the Application Specs, per `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document`, editing none of those files. Hand the application work it records on as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document` has a change needing both kinds split. Hand it to the application design this one was spawned from, or to the user, per `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Hand a change of its own to the user to spawn or log`. A follow-up logged for it names this design (`specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Follow-up`).

**Coin a term only once it passes the test.** Within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § State each step as its outcome`, apply the test in `specs/methodology/glossary.md § Writing an Entry` to each term a step would coin, and state each that passes in the step's Objectives. Within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Write the text in the form the rules give it`, write the term in the form that section gives. Use any abbreviation the term is given only as `specs/methodology/glossary.md § Using an Abbreviation` allows.

**Add or change a skill.** Within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § State each step as its outcome`, decide whether a skill the change adds is registered, by the test `specs/methodology/skills.md § Registered Skills` states. Within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Write the text in the form the rules give it`, write the skill in the form `specs/methodology/skills.md § Authoring a Skill` gives. A skill's instructions, and both `AGENTS.md` files, stay as `specs/methodology/scope.md § Agent Agnostic` asks.

**Draw no example from the application specs.** Within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Write the text in the form the rules give it`, follow the rule `specs/methodology/spec-style.md § Clear Prose` gives on an example in the canon.

**Keep the methodology's own files in sync.** Within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § State each step as its outcome`, name in the step's What and in the Target each of these that restates what the step changes. Within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Synchronize whatever restates what you changed`, keep them in sync:
- the methodology's `architecture.md` and `specs/architecture.md`, per `specs/methodology/sourcing-and-citation.md § Keeping Specs in Sync § Materialized Views`;
- `index.md`, per `specs/methodology/spec-placement.md § Where a File Goes`;
- the tree `specs/methodology/scope.md § The Shape of the Scope` draws;
- `specs/AGENTS.md § Writing specs` and the index it holds.

**Settle what the audit finds in the application specs as application work.** Within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Check what you wrote, then the post-apply review, the audit and the commit`, at settling the audit, and at settling a post-apply round for such a finding the post-apply review makes, settle each such finding by the Decision Table in `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document § Changes After Approval`, taking its rows in their order. Add each finding the table hands on to the application work the review handed on, or hand it on the same way, editing none of those files. Record it in the design's Impact on the Application Specs (`specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document`). Where it is logged as a follow-up, the follow-up names this design (`specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Follow-up`).

## What a canon change leaves behind

The application specs were written against the rule as it stood. A rule tightened, renamed or moved can leave them in breach, and nothing in the canon change itself shows it. Every canon file is correct, and the breach sits in files the change never touched. The audit script finds such a breach only where it checks the rule, a field's key or an Open Questions section's form among them. The audit's reading of the application specs at the apply, a run of `.ai/skills/audit-specs/` given `canon-specs`, which reads every audit whose Kind column names a kind of change, over the files of every kind but `code` (`specs/methodology/design-workflow.md § A Design's Reviews`), finds a breach only once the change is written. A review before the change is approved lets the application work be known, and handed on, while the canon design is still shaped.
