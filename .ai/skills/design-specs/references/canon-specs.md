# Designing the canon

These are the steps a change to the canon owes beyond the Workflow of `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md`. They are read from that Workflow's step Frame the change, once the design's Kind settles the change is the canon's, and again from its fork, in the order the Workflow reaches the last step each joins. Each names the step it joins, or, where it decides and writes, the deciding step and the writing step. Every other specification is written and checked against the canon's rules, so a change to those rules reaches further than it looks.

## Workflow

**Research the application specs the change reaches.** Within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Frame the change`, take the application specs into the research setting the frame, for the reason `§ What a canon change leaves behind` gives. Choose Searches over `specs/application/` for each rule the goals and the frame change, run by meaning as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § The Goals and the Frame` has the sweep run. Hand them to a cold agent as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Measure Twice, Cut Once § Research` has research handed off, reading none of those files yourself to set the design. An application area is assessed again only as that section has an area assessed again. Hold each application area the research assesses `changes` as an item of the design's Impact on the Application Specs, in the form `specs/methodology/working-files/main.md § A Design Document` gives an item, editing none of those files. Hand the application work each item holds on as `specs/methodology/working-files/main.md § A Design Document § A Change Needing Both Kinds` has it handed on: to the application design this one was spawned from, or to the user, per `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Hand a change of its own to the user to spawn or log`. A follow-up logged for it names this design (`specs/methodology/working-files/main.md § A Follow-up`).

**Find everything applying a rule before changing it.** Within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Find the fact's existing home`, research each place a change to the rule lands: the steps of the skills citing its section, the audits table and the Skipped Step records naming it in their Sections, the records of the checks standing under it, the rules building on it, and the labels in `.ai/skills/lib/` naming the section. ## What a canon change leaves behind

The application specs were written against the rule as it stood. A rule tightened, renamed or moved can leave them in breach, and nothing in the canon change itself shows it. Every canon file is correct, and the breach sits in files the change never touched. The audit script finds such a breach only where it checks the rule, a field's key or an `Open Questions` section's form among them. The audit's reading of the application specs at the apply, a run of `.ai/skills/audit-specs/` given `canon-specs`, reading what `specs/methodology/design-workflow/designs-reviews.md § A Design's Reviews` has such a run read, finds a breach only once the change is written. A review before the change is approved lets the application work be known, and handed on, while the canon design is still shaped.
