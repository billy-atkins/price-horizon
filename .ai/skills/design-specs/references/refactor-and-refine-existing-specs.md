# Refactoring and refining existing specs

The steps a Refactor and Refine cycle (`specs/methodology/glossary.md`) over the canon's specs or the application's owes beyond the Workflow of `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md`, read from its Scope step, in the order that Workflow reaches them. Each names the step it joins. Every change the cycle makes to what the specs state is Design, and so the user's steer, per `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine (DRR)`; a design still in flight is refactored and refined by its own DRR, never by this cycle.

## Workflow

**One kind, the whole or a part —** within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Scope the change`: a cycle covers one kind of specification, the canon's or the application's, since `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document` splits a change needing both, and covers all of that kind's specs or a part named in its Scope, a domain, say, or a rule's file and the skills citing it, its baseline and sweep covering that part. A request for a cycle over both kinds is two designs linked in the workstack: the canon's first, and the application's spawned by it and naming it in Depends On, so the application's is approved once the canon's is and applied after it, against the canon the canon's design leaves (`specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document`). Propose that pair to the user rather than one design over both.

**Start from a baseline commit —** within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Scope the change`: record in the design's Scope the commit the cycle starts from, never a copy of the specs, so every review diffs the work against it with `git diff <baseline> -- specs .ai/skills` and reads an original with `git show <baseline>:<path>`. Where the cycle carries out what canon designs handed on in their Impact on the Application Specs, its stub carries that hand-off whole, since a finished design is deleted once no design names it in its Depends On (`specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document`).

**Sweep the baseline, and wide —** within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Scope the change`: sweep the files as they stand at the baseline, read fresh rather than recalled from an earlier survey (`specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine (DRR)`), and read each placement rule for what its words make true of the files. For each file or section that moves or goes, find every citation of it, by its heading paths and by its file name alone, in `specs/`, in the skills and in the working files, a bare same-file citation in a working file included, and draw the move map before any edit: each citation, by file and line, and where it goes.

**Take each change to what the specs state to the user —** within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Record each steer and apply it`: content removed, an entry a gathered file held stated at its home, an open question's provisional answer, and a disagreement found between two passages each change what the specs state, which is Design, so each is the user's steer (`specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Steering Decision`); where a passage sits, how it is worded and in what construct, and how a moved citation reads, is Refactor and Refine, and stays craft.

**Check a gathered file entry by entry —** within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Find the fact's existing home`: before moving or removing a file that gathers what other files state, a table of constraints, say, a cold agent checks each entry against the home it cites, giving a verdict and a quote for each, since whether an entry is stated there is judgment. An entry its home states needs nothing; one a mechanism enforces but no home states is stated where the mechanism lives; and one nothing enforces is recorded as what it is, an open question at its home (`specs/methodology/spec-placement.md § An Open Question`) or the follow-up already holding it.

**Choose the construct again —** within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Check whether a construct applies before writing prose`: prose that has grown conditions is weighed again for the construct `specs/methodology/modeling-constructs.md § Purpose` and `specs/methodology/modeling-constructs.md § When to Use Which` give it; and prose adding conditions to a construct is folded into it, the construct chosen again where the combined logic fits another better, a Decision Table whose conditions turn out to depend on their order becoming a Decision Tree, say. A construct can expose a combination of conditions the baseline's text never settled, which is a decision about what the specs state: it goes to the user, or is recorded as an open question (`specs/methodology/spec-placement.md § An Open Question`), never filled in as craft. Whether the construct states exactly what the text it replaces stated is judgment, so a cold agent checks both ways against that text at the baseline: every outcome it gave survives, and the construct states none it did not; where the prose and the construct it joins disagreed, the disagreement goes to the user, never settled silently in the merge.

**Build moved text from the baseline —** within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Draft with the source file open`: a moved passage is copied from the baseline's own text, its headings raised or lowered as its new place needs, never retyped.

**Refine the prose —** within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Strip drafting residue`: where a passage can state the same in fewer or plainer words, or in an order a reader follows more easily, reword it, never changing what it states, and never so tersely that a reader must reconstruct what the words left out. Whether a rewording states what it did is judgment, so a cold agent compares each, taken from the design's own pairs of the text it changes and what that text becomes, with its text at the baseline, and confirms it states the same.

**Check citations at both trees —** within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Verify mechanically`: a move's design quotes old paths that resolve only at the baseline and new ones only once it is applied, so run `--check-cited-headings` on the design at both, the design's changes applied in a working tree; the citations broken at each are exact complements, the proof that every citation the design quotes was rewritten.

**Show what the cycle changed —** within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Refactor and refine, then the adversarial DRR, then apply and audit`: before each review, with the design's changes applied in a working tree, and again once they are applied, run `--show-refactor-and-refine-of-existing-specs <baseline>`, per `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Verifying a proposal`, and trace each line it lists: a line repeated is a second home unless it repeats by design, a diagram's back-citation, say; and each line lost or new traces to a steer, the move map, a rewording or a construct chosen again, a rewritten citation showing as one of each.
