# Refactoring and refining existing specs

These are the steps a Refactor and Refine cycle (`specs/methodology/glossary.md`) over the canon's specs or the application's owes beyond the Workflow of `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md`. They are read from that Workflow's step Frame the change, in the order the Workflow reaches the last step each joins. Each names the step it joins, or, where it decides and writes, the deciding step and the writing step. Every change the cycle makes to what the specs state is Design (`specs/methodology/glossary.md`), and so the user's steer (`specs/methodology/working-files.md § Steering Decision`). A design still in flight is refactored and refined by its own pass through the Design, Refactor, Refine process, never by this cycle.

## Workflow

**One kind, the whole or a part.** Within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Frame the change`, a cycle covers one kind of specification, the canon's or the application's, since `specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document` splits a change needing both. The cycle covers all of that kind's specs, or a part named in its design frame's Scope, such as a domain, or a rule's file and the skills citing it. Its baseline and sweep then cover that part. A request for a cycle over both kinds is two designs linked in the workstack. The canon's design comes first. The application's design is spawned by it and names it in Depends On, its frame's Scope stating what it relies on from the canon's, so the application's is approved once the canon's is, and applied after it, against the canon the canon's design leaves (`specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document`). Propose that pair to the user rather than one design over both, as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Decides` has a call asked.

**Start from a baseline commit.** Within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Frame the change`, record in the design frame's Scope the commit the cycle starts from, never a copy of the specs. Every review then diffs the work against it with `git diff <baseline> -- specs .ai/skills` and reads an original with `git show <baseline>:<path>`. Where the cycle carries out what canon designs handed on in their Impact on the Application Specs, its stub carries that hand-off whole, because a finished design is deleted once no design names it in its Depends On (`specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document`).

**Sweep the baseline, and wide.** Within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Frame the change`, sweep the files as they stand at the baseline, read fresh rather than recalled from an earlier survey (`specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Reading Fresh`). Read each placement rule for what its words make true of the files. For each file or section that moves or goes, find every citation of it, by its heading paths and by its file name alone. Search `specs/`, the skills and the working files, a bare same-file citation in a working file included. Draw the move map before stating the steps: each citation in the Target, by file and line, and where it goes. A citation in a working file the move leaves stale is the builder's to rewrite, as `specs/methodology/working-files.md § The Working Files` has a working file changed, and the design names none.

**Take each change to what the specs state to the user.** Within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Record each steer and apply it`, take these to the user: content removed, an entry a gathered file held stated at its home, an open question's provisional answer, and a disagreement found between two passages. Ask each as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Decides` has a call asked. Each changes what the specs state, which is Design, so each is the user's steer (`specs/methodology/working-files.md § Steering Decision`). Where a passage sits, how it is worded and in what construct, and how a moved citation reads, is Refactor and Refine, and stays craft.

**Check a gathered file entry by entry.** Within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Find the fact's existing home`, before a file that gathers what other files state, such as a table of constraints, is moved or removed, a cold agent checks each entry against the home the entry cites, giving a verdict and a quote for each. The check is a cold agent's because whether an entry is stated there is judgment, a judgment the builder hands off (`specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Checks What`). An entry its home states needs nothing. An entry a mechanism enforces but no home states is stated where the mechanism lives. An entry nothing enforces is recorded as what it is: an open question at its home (`specs/methodology/spec-placement.md § Open Question`), or work recorded in the design's Out of Scope (`specs/methodology/working-files.md § A Design Document`), handed on as `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Hand a change of its own to the user to spawn or log` has it unless a follow-up already holds it.

**Choose the construct again.** Within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Check whether a construct applies before writing prose`, weigh again, for the construct `specs/methodology/modeling-constructs.md § When to Use Which` gives it, prose that has grown conditions. Fold prose adding conditions to a construct into that construct. Choose the construct again where the combined logic fits another better. A Decision Table whose later conditions turn out to make sense only once an earlier one has been answered, say, becomes a First table, as `specs/methodology/modeling-constructs.md § Constructs § Decision Table` has a tree of questions. A construct can expose a combination of conditions the baseline's text never settled. That combination is a decision about what the specs state, so it goes to the user, or is recorded as an open question (`specs/methodology/spec-placement.md § Open Question`), never filled in as craft. Whether the construct states exactly what the text it replaces stated is judgment, a judgment the builder hands off (`specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Checks What`), so a cold agent checks both ways against that text at the baseline. It checks that every outcome the text gave survives, and that the construct states none the text did not. Where the prose and the construct it joins disagreed, the disagreement goes to the user, never settled silently in the merge. A combination or a disagreement taken to the user is asked as `specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Decides` has a call asked.

**Build moved text from the baseline.** Within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Write the text in the form the rules give it`, copy a moved passage from the baseline's own text, never retyping it, with its headings raised or lowered as its new place needs.

**Refine the prose.** Within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Strip drafting residue, and write clearly`, reword a passage that can state the same in plainer words, or in an order a reader follows more easily, toward the prose `specs/methodology/spec-style.md § Clear Prose` describes, never changing what it states. Split a sentence holding several rules, as `specs/methodology/sourcing-and-citation.md § One Home Per Fact § One Rule to a Sentence` has it. Whether a rewording states what the passage did is judgment, a judgment the builder hands off (`specs/AGENTS.md § Design, Refactor, Refine § Who Checks What`). A cold agent compares each rewording with its text at the baseline, and confirms it states the same. The agent runs at the apply, once the rewordings are written, and takes each from the diff of the written files against the baseline.

**Check citations at both trees.** Within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Verify mechanically`, run `--check-cited-headings` on the design before each validation, where a move's steps name old paths that resolve at the baseline. Within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Check what you wrote, then the post-apply review, the audit and the commit`, once every citation the move map lists is rewritten, run it on each file the apply wrote, where each citation then resolves.

**Show what the cycle changed.** Within `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Workflow § Check what you wrote, then the post-apply review, the audit and the commit`, run `--show-refactor-and-refine-of-existing-specs <baseline>`, per `.ai/skills/design-specs/SKILL.md § Verifying a proposal`. Run it at writing, once the design's changes are written and before the post-apply review, and again after each change written in answer to a finding or the user's feedback. Trace each line it lists. A line repeated is a second home unless it repeats by design, such as a diagram's back-citation. Each line lost or new traces to a steer, the move map, a rewording or a construct chosen again. A rewritten citation shows as one line lost and one line new.
